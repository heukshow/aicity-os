Choose HelpDesk when

You want a compact support stack with explicit AI-resolution allowances.

  • ✓ Shared inbox, ticketing, chat and automation are the core job.
  • ✓ You prefer to model AI cost around resolved customer questions.
  • ✓ Essential's lower entry price and included AI are enough for a small team.
  • ✓ You want to test the full workflow for 14 days before paying.

Choose Freshdesk when

You need the Freshdesk feature ladder and session-based Freddy AI model.

  • ✓ Customer portal and knowledge base are important from the Growth tier.
  • ✓ Pro-level multilingual helpdesk, custom dashboards or intelligent routing matter.
  • ✓ You prefer to model AI usage around Freddy AI Agent sessions.
  • ✓ You want to trial the official Freshdesk product before committing.

Do not compare the $19 headline alone

The entry annual price is currently the same, but AI units are not equivalent: HelpDesk counts AI resolutions, while Freshdesk sells additional Freddy AI Agent sessions. A low-cost test should use your real ticket mix for the full 14-day trial and record how many AI resolutions or sessions your team consumes before estimating monthly cost.

FAQ

Which starts cheaper?

Their current entry annual prices are tied at $19 per seat per month: HelpDesk Essential is priced per user and Freshdesk Growth per agent. Feature and AI usage differences become more important than the headline price.

Can I test both without a card?

Both vendors currently advertise a 14-day free trial without a credit card. Recheck the live signup terms before starting because trial conditions can change.
