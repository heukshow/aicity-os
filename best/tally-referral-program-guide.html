Tally referral program: how the 50% 3-month benefit works

Tally's official referral program currently says an eligible new user who signs up through a personal referral link and later becomes a paying customer receives 50% off the subscription for three months. This is a personal referral benefit. It is not a public Tally sale, coupon code or sitewide promotion.

Eligibility & program safety

Tally states that referrals apply to new signups that later upgrade to a paid plan. Existing users are not eligible, self-referrals are prohibited, and paid ads containing a Tally referral link are prohibited. COSHUMA presents the link only as a clearly disclosed personal referral route and does not describe it as a public sale.
