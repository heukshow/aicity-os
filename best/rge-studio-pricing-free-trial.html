Buyer Decision Box

Fast answer

Choose RGE Studio if…
  • ✓ You already have an ESP/CRM but dislike its email builder.
  • ✓ You need reusable templates, brand controls, review or approvals.
  • ✓ You want to export or push finished designs into another platform.
  • ✓ Multiple people need to collaborate on email production.
Skip or stay on Starter if…
  • ✓ You only build a few emails or pages each month.
  • ✓ Your current email platform's native editor is good enough.
  • ✓ You do not need structured approvals or brand governance.
  • ✓ Your priority is sending/automation rather than design production.

Why teams pay instead of staying on Starter

The strongest upgrade reasons are not simply “more templates.” Beefree's latest Ambassador materials emphasize collaboration, commenting/review, brand controls, connectors to sending platforms and AI-assisted creation. The live pricing page also positions Business around workflows where production speed, brand consistency and approvals matter.

Collaboration: co-editing, review and approval reduce back-and-forth when more than one person touches an email.
Brand control: reusable content and governance features help teams keep campaigns consistent.
ESP/CRM compatibility: RGE Studio can act as the design layer while the sending system remains elsewhere.
AI assistance: current product materials include AI writing/design support, with plan-specific limits and features.
