When Free is probably enough

  • Your site stays below roughly 10,000 monthly pageviews.
  • You want to test popup targeting, personalization or experiments before committing money.
  • You only need one domain during evaluation.
  • You prefer to validate conversion lift before choosing a paid traffic tier.

When to look at paid tiers

Upgrade pressure is mostly traffic-driven. If your site outgrows the Free pageview allowance or you need more domains, check the official pricing selector at the time of purchase because paid rates change with billing period and traffic level.
