Stay free when…

  • Your monthly popup/form audience is still within the current 1,000-visitor allowance.
  • You are validating campaign design, triggers and lead capture.
  • You have not measured enough conversion lift to justify recurring software spend.

Upgrade when…

  • Your traffic exceeds the free visitor limit consistently.
  • You need more domains, account management or higher operating limits.
  • Your measured conversion value exceeds the incremental subscription cost.
