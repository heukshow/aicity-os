Choose Starter if…

  • You are below 2,500 subscribers.
  • You can stay within 10,000 monthly sends.
  • You only need one landing page and one form for now.
  • You prefer no-card testing over starting a paid trial.

Consider Pro if…

  • You need to remove EmailOctopus branding.
  • You need unlimited landing pages/forms or more team access.
  • You need reports retained beyond 30 days.
