Quick decision

Which one should you pick?

  • Choose BoldSign if sending volume and predictable eSignature pricing matter most.
  • Choose Dropbox Sign if Dropbox is already central to your document workflow.
  • Choose PandaDoc if proposals, sales docs, and signatures need to live in the same workflow.

What to compare before paying

Document volume

Check whether the plan limits envelopes, documents, users, templates, or API requests. A low headline price can be less attractive if your real usage triggers limits quickly.

Team and admin controls

Look at role management, reporting, reusable templates, branding, signer authentication, and whether adding users changes the economics.

Integrations

The right eSignature tool should fit where documents already live. Switching between apps can erase the time savings you expected from electronic signatures.

Compliance and procurement

If your organization has specific legal, identity, retention, or procurement requirements, verify those directly with the vendor before switching platforms.
