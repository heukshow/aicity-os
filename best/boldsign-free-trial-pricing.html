Buyer decision

Free plan, trial or paid plan?

Stay on Essential when

Your monthly send volume is small enough for the current free allowance and you do not need the higher-tier team, template or workflow features.

Use the 30-day trial when

You need to evaluate a real team workflow, recurring templates, reminders, authentication or higher-volume sending before committing budget.

Upgrade when

The paid tier saves more staff time or signature-chasing cost than its subscription price and its envelope/user limits match your normal workload.

BoldSign is a stronger fit when

  • ✓ You want to replace print-sign-scan and manual signature chasing.
  • ✓ Contracts, HR onboarding or approvals repeat often enough for templates and reminders to matter.
  • ✓ Cost predictability and envelope volume matter more than staying with a legacy e-signature brand.
  • ✓ You want to test the workflow without entering a card first.

Compare alternatives first when

  • ✓ Procurement already requires a different e-signature vendor.
  • ✓ A specific regional identity, compliance or integration requirement is mandatory.
  • ✓ Your workflow depends on an existing Docusign-specific integration or contract.
  • ✓ The free plan already covers your actual monthly usage.

Use the trial as a four-step decision test

1. Send

Upload one real agreement or onboarding document and prepare it for signature.

2. Sign

Complete the signer experience and inspect authentication, reminders and audit trail.

3. Repeat

Turn the document into a reusable workflow and check whether the repeated process is actually faster.

4. Decide

Only pay when the time saved and required limits justify the paid tier for your normal volume.
