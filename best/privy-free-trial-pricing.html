A useful 15-day test plan

  1. Connect the store. Verify catalog/customer data and consent settings before importing everything.
  2. Build one capture campaign. Test a popup or embedded form on mobile and desktop.
  3. Run one revenue workflow. Validate a welcome or cart-recovery sequence rather than building many campaigns.
  4. Measure list size and cost. Check how your actual mailable-contact count changes the paid Email price.
  5. Choose the narrowest product. If you only need onsite capture, compare the separate Pop-ups & Displays option before buying the broader Email/SMS stack.

When Omnisend may be the lower-risk start

Privy gives you a time-limited no-card trial. Omnisend instead offers a permanent free tier for a small contact base. If you are not ready for a paid subscription after 15 days, compare the two before migrating your audience.
