A useful 14-day test plan

  • Publish one campaign on real traffic rather than only previewing templates.
  • Check mobile layout, triggers, segmentation and frequency controls.
  • Connect your actual email or ecommerce stack.
  • Measure signups, clicks or another conversion step you already track.
  • Compare visitor-tier cost only after the workflow proves useful.

Pricing caution

Sleeknote prices by monthly visitors and billing frequency. The current entry display is $69/month on monthly billing or $55/month on annual billing for 0–25,000 monthly visitors. Use the vendor pricing page immediately before any upgrade.
