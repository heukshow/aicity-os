A simple test before upgrading

  1. Pick one real product and one target audience.
  2. Create one short video-ad concept and a small set of image variants within the free allowance.
  3. Judge script quality, actor fit, brand consistency and editing effort—not just generation speed.
  4. Only compare paid plans after you know the workflow saves enough production time for your use case.
