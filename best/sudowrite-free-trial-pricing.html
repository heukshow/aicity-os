What you are paying for

Fiction-specific tools, not just a blank chatbot

Write

Continues a story in Auto or Guided mode when you are stuck or know the direction but want drafting help.

Rewrite

Rephrases existing passages, shortens text, makes it more descriptive, or applies other revision styles while preserving the underlying idea.

Brainstorm & Describe

Generates plot, character and worldbuilding ideas, while Describe focuses on sensory detail for scenes and objects.

Draft & Scenes

Structures scenes and can generate longer chapter drafts from outlines and scene instructions, aimed at long-form fiction workflows.
