#2 · Best for text-based editing of recorded media

Descript

Descript offers a $0 Free plan. Its current annual plan view lists Creator at $12 per editor/month and Pro at $24 per editor/month. It is strongest when you already have recordings and want transcription-driven video/audio editing, screen recording, captions, filler-word cleanup and AI voice tools in the same editor.

Best for
Podcasts, interviews, screen recordings, YouTube editing and transcript-first workflows
Watch for
Media/transcription allowances vary by plan and editor seat

#3 · Best free script-to-video experiment

Fliki

Fliki's official pricing page currently offers a free plan with no credit card, 3 credits per month, 300 voices across 80+ languages, 720p exports and script/blog/PPT-to-video workflows. Paid plan prices can vary with monthly/yearly selection, so this guide sends buyers to the official plan page for current checkout pricing.
