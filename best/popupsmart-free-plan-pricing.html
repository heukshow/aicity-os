When the free plan is enough

  • You are validating whether popups actually fit your site and audience.
  • You need to test design, targeting and lead capture before committing budget.
  • Your traffic and campaign-volume requirements remain within the current free-tier limits.

When to consider upgrading

  • Your campaigns consistently exceed the free-tier capacity.
  • You need more advanced targeting, campaign scale or operational headroom.
  • Your measured conversion lift is large enough to justify the monthly cost.

Do not upgrade just because a promotional discount exists. Measure the actual campaign benefit first.
