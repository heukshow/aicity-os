Start free when…

  • You are testing whether shortcuts actually save time.
  • You are a single user and 50 weekly uses are enough.
  • You want to validate your template library before paying.

Consider Premium when…

  • You regularly exceed the free usage allowance.
  • Your team needs shared templates and consistent replies.
  • You need Premium features such as integrations, image uploads or webhooks.
