Trial billing checklist

  • Trial length: 14 days for Create and Optimize.
  • Credit card: required at signup.
  • Trial traffic limit: 2,500 unique visitors.
  • If the trial ends without cancellation, the selected paid plan is charged.
  • Reaching the trial traffic limit can end the trial early and transition the account to the selected subscription.
