Do not treat “free trial” as “no billing risk”

Landingi's official pricing FAQ says a valid credit card may sometimes be requested. It also says that after the trial, the selected paid subscription can start unless canceled. Before starting, verify the card requirement, selected plan, taxes, exact first-charge date and cancellation path shown on your own signup screen.

Which plan should you test?

  • Build: start here if your main job is publishing a modest number of campaign landing pages.
  • Optimize: test this when experimentation, EventTracker and deeper CRO analysis are central to the purchase.
  • Scale: evaluate this when you manage high page volume, multiple client workflows or programmatic landing pages.
