Who gets the most value?

Strong fit
  • Marketing or revenue teams with data spread across multiple tools
  • Teams that repeatedly answer the same KPI and reporting questions
  • Operators who want natural-language analysis on governed business metrics

Check before paying

Best way to evaluate Databox Genie

Use one real question, one real connected dataset and one recurring reporting task. If Genie gives a useful answer and reduces manual reporting work, then choose the plan based on users, data sources, MCP needs and monthly AI credits—not on the AI label alone.
