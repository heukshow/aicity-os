A useful 14-day test plan

  • Launch at least one real popup or lead-capture workflow.
  • Verify audience targeting and mobile behavior on your own traffic.
  • Connect the email/SMS or ecommerce integration you actually use.
  • Measure whether the workflow improves a conversion step you care about.
  • Only compare paid cost after the product proves useful for your site.

Pricing caution

Justuno's pricing is traffic- and billing-sensitive. The current page displays Lite at $59/month in its annual-save context, but the amount you see at purchase may differ. Use the vendor pricing page immediately before any upgrade.

Compare a permanent-free alternative

OptiMonk currently offers a permanent $0 plan capped at 10,000 pageviews per month. If you need more time than a 14-day evaluation, compare the two entry models first.
