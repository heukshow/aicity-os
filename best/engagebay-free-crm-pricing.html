When to remain on Free

  • Your test dataset remains below 250 contacts.
  • One landing page and basic branded email capacity are sufficient.
  • You are still validating whether one suite reduces manual handoffs.

When to compare paid tiers

Compare Basic, Growth and Pro only when contact volume, automation, reporting or administrative controls exceed the free limits. EngageBay shows different effective prices by billing term; the live checkout is authoritative.
