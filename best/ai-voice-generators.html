#1 · Best overall for creators & flexible voice workflows

ElevenLabs

The current Creative plans start at Free ($0), Starter ($6/month), Creator ($22/month with the first month shown at $11), Pro ($99/month), Scale ($299/month) and Business ($990/month). The free tier includes 10k monthly credits; Starter adds a commercial license and instant voice cloning.

Best for
Creators, multilingual voice production, voice cloning, API experimentation
Watch for
Credit usage varies by product/model; high-volume plans get expensive quickly

#2 · Best for structured business voiceovers

Murf AI

Murf's current yearly-billed Studio pricing shows Free at $0, Creator at $19/month, Business at $66/month and Enterprise as custom. The free tier requires no credit card; paid Creator includes unlimited downloads and commercial rights.

Best for
Business voiceovers, guided studio work, teams that value a conventional editing workflow
