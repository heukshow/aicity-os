Free vs paid: when upgrading actually makes sense

Stay free if…

  • • You are validating answer quality on a small documentation set.
  • • One bot and 50 source pages are enough for the test.
  • • You do not yet need actions, scheduling or workflow integrations.

Consider paid if…

  • • Your source library exceeds the free-page limit.
  • • 100 monthly AI credits are too small for real traffic.
  • • You need lead forms, actions, integrations, analytics or multiple users.
