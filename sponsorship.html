Sponsored placement terms

Updated October 10, 2026

Applications are open. Review the placement rates, copy guide and application. An application is a request for review; it does not reserve a page, take payment or activate an advertisement.

What you can buy

A sponsored placement is one clearly labeled text card on an approved COSHUMA tool, buyer-guide or comparison page, for the confirmed duration. The card contains a headline, description and linked button. Logos, image banners and videos are not part of these packages. Rates are one-time USD amounts; there is no automatic renewal. For the current F1–F3 image booking flow, an advertiser with a verified paid placement that is still active has first access to renew that same placement for the immediately following period.

Applications for longer periods require a separate quote. We confirm the exact page, duration, price and order reference before payment. Availability is subject to review and existing reservations.

Copy review and editorial independence

You must be authorized to promote the product and use the submitted copy and destination. Claims must be accurate and supportable. We may request corrections or decline unsuitable, misleading or unrelated advertising. Submission or payment alone does not mean copy approval.

Every paid card is labeled Sponsored. Buying a placement does not buy an editorial rating, a recommendation, an organic ranking or a change to our comparison criteria. Impressions and outbound clicks may be reported, but leads, signups, customers, sales and revenue are not guaranteed.

Order identity and payment confirmation

Provide the company name and tool or product name in your application. A saved online application receives a unique COSHUMA order reference. When discussing or identifying a payment, use order reference / company name / tool name. A payer's display name, payment screenshot or approval screen alone is not confirmation of payment.

Use PayPal only through the issued order when checkout is available. COSHUMA checks the completed payment against the order, expected amount and currency before activation. If checkout is unavailable, submit your application or email support@coshuma.com and wait for order instructions. Do not send an unsolicited transfer.

Activation, duration and renewal

Your paid period begins when the approved placement actually goes live, rather than when the application or payment is received. A verified payment, approved copy, an available location and a confirmed start and end time are all required. The card stops at the agreed end time. While a verified paid placement is active, its advertiser may reserve the immediately following period before that inventory reopens to new applicants. The renewal is a separate confirmed period and payment, and no charge happens automatically. If the advertiser does not complete the renewal before the current paid period ends, the position may reopen.

A refunded or reversed payment, a payment dispute, or a material problem with the advertisement may pause or stop the placement while the issue is reviewed.

Cancellation and refund requests

Before a placement goes live, you may request cancellation and a full refund. If COSHUMA cannot provide the purchased placement, you may request a full refund. After activation, requests to adjust the unused portion of the period are reviewed individually.

Send your order reference, company name, tool name and reason to support@coshuma.com. We review the order, payment and actual delivery before arranging the applicable refund or adjustment. Submitting a request does not itself execute a refund. These terms do not limit mandatory consumer or other applicable legal rights.

Application information

We use your business details, contact email, copy and destination to review and operate the placement, and retain the minimum order information needed to verify payment and resolve support requests. Contact and payment details are not included in the public sponsored card. See the privacy policy.
