## English copy for LinkedIn

Most sales managers don’t need to watch every call.
They need to know which moments are worth coaching.

When only a small sample of calls gets reviewed, coaching can easily become subjective. Objection handling, talk balance, next steps, and whether the team actually followed the sales process can all get missed.

That’s why AI call review is interesting to us: not as a replacement for a sales manager, but as a way to surface the moments that deserve attention first.

For a sales team, the useful question isn’t “Did we record the call?” It’s “What should the manager coach from this call?”

How many of your team’s calls did you actually review last week?
