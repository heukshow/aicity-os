Last updated: September 17, 2026

Service

COSHUMA offers clearly labeled sponsored placements for relevant AI and SaaS products. Sponsored placements are operated separately from COSHUMA editorial ratings, reviews, comparisons and organic rankings.

Checkout status

The public sponsorship checkout remains temporarily unavailable while the updated payment and campaign system is being verified. Do not attempt to pay through an old or cached checkout route. COSHUMA will reopen checkout only after the payment, campaign, placement and reporting flow has passed its release checks.

Review, availability and placement

Payment does not guarantee acceptance, a specific page or exact position, or any editorial rating. COSHUMA reviews submitted materials for relevance, accuracy, safety and fit. A requested page must match the purchased placement type and the requested campaign flight must not conflict with an existing sponsored placement. If COSHUMA accepts payment but cannot provide an eligible placement, the refund policy applies.

Campaign timing

The purchased campaign duration begins when the sponsored placement is scheduled to go live, not when payment is made. A future requested start date may be used when available. Active placements automatically stop at the recorded campaign end time.

Reporting

COSHUMA reports recorded sponsored impressions, sponsored clicks and click-through rate (CTR). COSHUMA does not estimate advertiser signups, purchases or advertiser revenue unless a separate verified measurement integration is explicitly established.

Affiliate separation

Affiliate links are operated separately. A sponsorship purchase neither creates an affiliate relationship nor changes ordinary directory or editorial links.

What a sponsor must provide

Before publication, a sponsor must provide accurate business and product details, an HTTPS destination URL, an HTTPS logo URL, campaign copy, CTA text, an eligible COSHUMA target page and confirmation that the submitted materials are accurate and authorized for use. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, API keys or sensitive personal data.

Contact

For sponsorship questions, email support@coshuma.com. Do not send card, bank, PayPal login or account passwords by email.
