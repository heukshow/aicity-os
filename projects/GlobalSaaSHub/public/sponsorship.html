Automated fulfillment

After a verified payment, COSHUMA creates a campaign record and provides a private advertiser intake link. Sponsors submit company and product details, a destination URL, logo URL, headline, description, CTA text, requested start date, and placement-specific details. Automated validation checks required fields, HTTPS destinations, basic claim risk, and creative length. Campaigns that need human review remain pending and are not automatically published.

Reporting

Each paid campaign receives a private report link. COSHUMA reports only metrics it directly verifies, including sponsored impressions, sponsored clicks, CTR, campaign dates, placement and status. Signups, purchases, advertiser revenue or other downstream conversions are not inferred unless separately verified through a connected attribution source.

Review and placement

Payment does not guarantee acceptance, publication, a particular organic position, or any editorial rating. We review submissions for relevance, accuracy, safety, and fit. Sponsored placement will be identified clearly and will not alter independent comparison or rating criteria. If COSHUMA declines a submission or cannot provide the purchased placement, the refund policy applies.

Affiliate separation

Affiliate links are operated separately. A sponsorship purchase neither creates an affiliate relationship nor changes the destination of ordinary directory links.

What a sponsor must provide

Before publication, a sponsor must provide accurate campaign materials they have the right to use. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, government IDs, or sensitive personal data.

Checkout and contact

Software companies can choose a placement and duration at the secure COSHUMA checkout. Do not send payment information by email.
