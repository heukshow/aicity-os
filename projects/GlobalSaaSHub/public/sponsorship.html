COSHUMA Sponsorship Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Service

COSHUMA offers clearly labeled sponsored placements for relevant AI and SaaS products. The standard tool-page package is currently listed at USD 49 for 30 days from activation. Buyer-intent, comparison, shorter test, longer-duration and custom placements are separate packages and must be confirmed before payment.

Checkout status

The public sponsorship checkout is temporarily unavailable while payment handling is being verified. Do not attempt to pay through an old or cached checkout route. Contact COSHUMA first to confirm fit, availability, final price and the current approved payment method.

Review and placement

Payment does not guarantee acceptance, a specific page or exact position, or any editorial rating. COSHUMA reviews submissions for relevance, accuracy, safety and fit. Sponsored placement is identified clearly and does not alter independent comparison or rating criteria. If COSHUMA accepts payment but cannot provide the agreed placement, the refund policy applies.

Affiliate separation

Affiliate links are operated separately. A sponsorship purchase neither creates an affiliate relationship nor changes ordinary directory or editorial links.

What a sponsor must provide

Before publication, a sponsor may be asked for a product URL, business contact, accurate product description and promotional assets they have the right to use. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, API keys or sensitive personal data.

Contact

Send the product name, website, category, preferred placement, preferred duration and campaign goal. COSHUMA will confirm the current safe payment route only after the placement is reviewed. Do not send payment information by email.
