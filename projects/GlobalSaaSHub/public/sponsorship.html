COSHUMA Sponsorship Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Service

COSHUMA offers a standard one-time USD 49 sponsorship for one relevant product-page sponsored placement displayed for 30 days from activation. Secure checkout is currently available on the COSHUMA home page through PayPal, with a card option shown when PayPal makes card funding eligible for the buyer. Buyer-intent, comparison, shorter test, longer-duration and custom placements are separate packages and should be confirmed before payment.

Review and placement

Payment for the standard package does not guarantee acceptance, a specific product page or exact position, or any editorial rating. We review submissions for relevance, accuracy, safety, and fit. If accepted, the standard purchased placement runs for 30 days from actual activation. Sponsored placement will be identified clearly and will not alter independent comparison or rating criteria. If COSHUMA declines a submission or cannot provide the purchased placement, the refund policy applies.

Affiliate separation

Affiliate links are operated separately. A sponsorship purchase neither creates an affiliate relationship nor changes the destination of ordinary directory links.

What a sponsor must provide

Before publication, a sponsor may be asked for a product URL, business contact, accurate product description, and promotional assets they have the right to use. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, or sensitive personal data.

Checkout and contact

Software companies can use the secure USD 49 sponsorship checkout on the COSHUMA home page for the standard 30-day tool-page package. Contact COSHUMA first if you need to confirm fit, timing, a particular page, or a different placement package. Do not send payment information by email.
