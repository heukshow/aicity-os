Cost watch

Credits matter more than the headline plan price

Make describes each module action in a scenario as a credit in its core pricing explanation. Multi-step scenarios therefore consume more credits per business event than a one-step workflow. Estimate cost using the scenario you will actually run.

Make's current pricing FAQ also says unused credits expire at the end of the term. Extra credits can cost more than credits included in a plan, making right-sizing more important than simply choosing the cheapest tier.

Which plan is worth paying for?

Core

The practical first paid tier. Choose it when minute-level scheduling, unlimited active scenarios or API access solves a real Free-plan bottleneck.

Pro

Worth the step up when execution priority, custom variables or deeper execution-log search matters operationally.

Teams

Buy for collaboration controls—not just because more than one person uses automation. Team roles and shared templates are the differentiators.

Enterprise

Use sales-assisted pricing only when enterprise apps, advanced security, 24/7 support or overage protection justify it.
