InVideo AI review & buyer guide

InVideo AI is designed for people who want to move from a prompt to a finished social or marketing video with less manual editing. It can generate scripts, scenes, voiceovers, captions and transitions, while its newer browser editor adds AI-assisted editing on a multitrack timeline.

What the free plan is good for

InVideo's July 2026 help documentation says the free plan uses limited credits, requires no card, and resets its free quota weekly on Monday at 12 a.m. UTC. That makes it useful for testing the workflow, but not a reliable proxy for the monthly volume of a paid production plan.

  • Test prompt-to-video and image-generation flows before paying.
  • Confirm whether the generated script, visual style and editing workflow fit your content.
  • Check model credit costs before committing to a monthly plan.
