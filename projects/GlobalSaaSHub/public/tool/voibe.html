Voibe FAQ

How much is Voibe?

The official site currently lists $7.50/month, $59/year and a $149 lifetime option for individual users. Promotional pricing and lifetime availability can change.

Does the 7-day trial require a card?

Voibe's dedicated trial help says downloading the app does not require a card, but starting the 7-day Pro Monthly or Annual trial through Lemon Squeezy does put a card on file. It says the card is not charged until day 8, and cancelling before then means no charge.

Is Voibe fully offline?

On supported Apple Silicon Macs, Voibe offers an on-device mode that runs locally. Windows and Intel Mac users use Voibe's zero-retention cloud mode.

Should I buy annual or lifetime?

Annual reduces subscription cost versus monthly, while lifetime removes recurring payments if you expect to keep using the product. Test it first, then compare the live prices because promotional pricing and lifetime availability may change.
