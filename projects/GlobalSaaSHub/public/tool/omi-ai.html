WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

Open-source wearable rather than only a meeting bot

Omi's official developer documentation says its hardware, firmware, app and backend are open source. The platform supports live transcription, conversation memory, summaries, an app marketplace, APIs/MCP and custom audio processing.

That makes Omi more attractive to developers and users who want a wearable capture layer they can customize, while conventional meeting assistants may be simpler when you only need scheduled online-meeting notes.

Omi AI pricing FAQ

Is there a free Omi subscription?

Yes. The latest Omi subscription help page lists Basic as free with 300 cloud listening minutes per month. The product page also describes free on-phone transcription.

Do I have to stop recording when I use all free minutes?

Omi says no: recording can continue, while the monthly allowance controls how much cloud transcription and analysis is processed. Saved audio can still be managed and exported.

What is the cheapest paid plan?

The current Plus plan is $19/month and includes 1,500 listening minutes per month. Annual billing is currently listed at $161.91.

Who needs Unlimited?

Heavy daily users who expect to exceed 1,500 processed minutes per month should compare Unlimited at the current $29/month price with Plus before subscribing.
