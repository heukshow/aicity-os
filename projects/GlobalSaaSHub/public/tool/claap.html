Claap Review (2026)

Its current product pages emphasize bot-free recording, multilingual transcription, AI summaries and follow-ups, CRM enrichment, call coaching, deal insights and MCP access for tools such as Claude and ChatGPT.

What Claap actually does

✓ Capture online and offline sales conversations, including bot-free desktop recording options
✓ Transcribe conversations in 99+ languages and turn them into summaries and action points
✓ Draft follow-up emails and update CRM notes, fields and deal context automatically
✓ Score calls, surface objections and provide AI coaching for sales managers and reps
✓ Search across meeting context and ask questions about deals, calls and pipeline signals
✓ Use Claap MCP to expose structured sales context to Claude, ChatGPT and compatible AI agents

Who should shortlist Claap?

Strong fit if...

  • Your reps lose time writing notes and updating CRM after calls.
  • You want meeting data to feed coaching, qualification and deal-review workflows.
  • You dislike visible meeting bots and prefer native capture options.
  • You want sales context available to AI agents through MCP.

Probably overkill if...

  • You only need basic transcription with no CRM or revenue workflow.
  • Your team does not run enough customer calls to justify a sales-intelligence layer.
  • You need a simple asynchronous video creator rather than meeting and deal intelligence.

Pricing: what to check before choosing a plan

Claap's official pricing pages currently separate lighter contributor/team use from revenue-team features such as CRM auto-complete, AI-generated emails, coaching, deal insights, Smart Tables and administrative controls. The site also shows annual billing discounts and free or trial entry paths. Because Claap is actively iterating its pricing pages, COSHUMA does not hard-code a checkout price here; confirm the live amount, currency, seat minimums and included AI credits on Claap before purchase.

COSHUMA verdict

Claap is most compelling when your real problem is not recording meetings but turning conversations into revenue operations: cleaner CRM data, faster follow-ups, coaching signals and reusable context for AI agents. If you only want a transcript, simpler tools may be enough. If you want the call to automatically become structured sales data, Claap deserves a test.
