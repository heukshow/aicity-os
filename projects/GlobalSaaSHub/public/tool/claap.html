What Claap actually does

✓ Capture online, offline and asynchronous conversations, including bot-free desktop recording options
✓ Transcribe conversations in 99+ languages and turn them into summaries and action points
✓ Draft follow-up emails and update CRM notes, fields and deal context automatically
✓ Score calls, surface objections and provide AI coaching for sales managers and reps
✓ Search across meeting context and ask questions about deals, calls and pipeline signals
✓ Use Claap MCP to expose structured sales context to Claude, ChatGPT and compatible AI agents

Who should shortlist Claap?

Strong fit if...

  • Your reps lose time writing notes and updating CRM after calls.
  • You want meeting data to feed coaching, qualification and deal-review workflows.
  • You want meeting recording and asynchronous video review in the same collaboration layer.
  • You want sales context available to AI agents through MCP.

Probably overkill if...

  • You only need basic transcription with no CRM or revenue workflow.
  • Your team does not run enough customer calls to justify a sales-intelligence layer.
  • Your workflow rarely involves sales calls, CRM updates, coaching, or collaborative async video review.

Current pricing guidance: what buyers should compare

Claap affiliate manager Lamia Karmaly supplied COSHUMA with current US-facing guidance of Free / Pro / Business / Enterprise, with Pro at $40/license/month or $32 with annual billing, Business at $75/license/month or $60 annual, and Enterprise custom. Claap's live pricing pages can render localized or revised plan labels and currencies, so use these figures as buyer guidance and verify the final live checkout terms before purchase.

Integrations and security buyers should check

Revenue-stack integrations

Claap's current official integrations include HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Slack and Notion, with Zapier and other automation routes available. This matters if you want call context to flow into CRM, collaboration and workflow automation rather than staying in a standalone transcript.

Security posture

Claap's official site states SOC 2 Type 2 certification and GDPR compliance, with data-residency options. Enterprise buyers should still confirm SSO, provisioning, access controls, audit requirements and the data-residency option that applies to their purchase.

COSHUMA verdict

Claap is most compelling when your real problem is not recording meetings but turning conversations into revenue operations: cleaner CRM data, faster follow-ups, coaching signals and reusable context for AI agents. It also supports asynchronous recording, so do not rule it out merely because async video is part of your workflow. If you only want a transcript, simpler tools may be enough. If you want the call to automatically become structured sales data, Claap deserves a test.
