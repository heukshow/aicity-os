AI agents: paid plans can run multiple agents with workspace knowledge, tools and automations.

Choose Taskade if...

  • You want AI agents, app generation and automation in one workspace.
  • You prefer a free starting point before committing to a paid plan.
  • Your team can benefit from a flat plan that includes multiple users rather than separate per-seat tools.
  • You want generated tools to stay connected to shared workspace knowledge.

Check carefully before upgrading if...

  • You do not expect to use AI generation enough to justify recurring credits.
  • You only need a conventional task manager rather than AI apps and agents.
  • You need a specific enterprise control such as SSO, custom domains or organization-wide provisioning.
  • Your usage is credit-heavy, because complex builds can consume materially more credits than simple prompts.
