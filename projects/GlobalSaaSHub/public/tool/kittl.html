Kittl pricing FAQ

Can I use Kittl for free?

Yes. Kittl's official site currently lists a $0 free plan. Kittl says the free plan is intended for exploring the editor and personal-use projects.

Does Kittl Pro include commercial use?

Kittl's current pricing FAQ says Pro and Expert include a commercial license, while the free plan is for personal use. Check the current license terms before publishing or selling a specific asset.

Can I cancel a paid Kittl plan?

Kittl says subscriptions can be cancelled from account settings and access continues through the paid billing period.

Which plan is the practical starting point?

Start free if you are evaluating the editor. Pro is the more practical paid tier for most solo commercial creators; Expert makes more sense when AI usage, storage and premium assets materially affect your workflow.

Ready to test Kittl?

The official site says Kittl is free to start and does not require a credit card for the free experience.
