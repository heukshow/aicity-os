Verified revenue alternative

AWeber is worth comparing when…

  • ✓ You need subscriber management, broadcasts and automated email sequences.
  • ✓ Landing pages, signup forms and campaign delivery should live in one account.
  • ✓ You want a small-business email platform rather than a standalone design-production layer.
  • ✓ Your buying decision depends on list size and monthly send capacity.
