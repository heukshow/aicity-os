What Advanced Listening changes

Advanced Listening continuously scans public conversations about keywords, phrases and profiles across supported social sources plus blogs, forums and news. Competitive listening can compare brands across Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, Reddit and the web, with mention volume and share-of-voice analysis.

Use Agorapulse when

  • You also need publishing, inbox and team workflows.
  • Listening should sit beside your daily social operations.
  • You want competitive listening inside the same platform.

Compare Brand24 when

  • Monitoring is the main job rather than social publishing.
  • You want a dedicated 14-day listening trial before committing.
  • AI visibility across ChatGPT-style surfaces also matters.

Choose by workflow, not feature count

Start Agorapulse if you need an all-in-one social management workflow. If the purchase decision is mainly about monitoring brands and competitors, use the dedicated comparison to test Brand24 and Agorapulse on the same real keyword set.
