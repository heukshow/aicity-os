What to validate during the trial

  • Whether your ecommerce platform and customer data sync cleanly into Privy.
  • Whether popup capture, welcome messages and abandoned-cart workflows fit the campaigns you actually need.
  • How your mailable-contact count affects the Email plan cost before importing a full list.
  • Whether you need the full Email/SMS stack or the separate Pop-ups & Displays only option.

Privy or Omnisend?

If your priority is a permanent $0 starting tier for ecommerce email, compare Omnisend. If your priority is testing Privy's onsite capture plus email/SMS workflow together, use Privy's no-card trial.
