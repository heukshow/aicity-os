Which tier is the rational starting point?

Start lower when…
  • One person is evaluating dashboards and AI analysis.
  • Five or fewer data sources cover the first workflow.
  • You want proof of time savings before adding collaboration capacity.
Move to Team or Agency when…
  • Multiple teammates need direct access.
  • You need more sources or a larger shared AI-credit pool.
  • You manage reporting across multiple client accounts.
