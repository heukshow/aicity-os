Docusign pricing & buyer guide

Official product and pricing pages checked September 11, 2026.

Docusign is a mature eSignature choice for individuals and teams that want reusable templates, agreement storage, audit trails and broad integrations. The practical buying question is whether its envelope limits and per-user pricing fit your sending volume.

Personal
$10/mo

Annual billing; 5 envelopes per month.

Standard
$25/user/mo

Annual billing; 100 envelopes per user per year.

Business Pro
$40/user/mo

Annual billing; 100 envelopes per user per year plus more advanced agreement features.

What to check before paying

Envelope volume

Personal lists 5 envelopes per month. Standard and Business Pro list 100 envelopes per user per year on the current US annual-billing view.

Team cost

Standard and Business Pro are priced per user, so compare the total team cost instead of only the headline monthly amount.

Free trial

Docusign says no credit card is required for the free trial, so test your real sending workflow before committing.

Workflow fit

If procurement, integrations or an existing Docusign ecosystem matter more than raw sending cost, switching may create more work than it saves.
