Choose HelpDesk when…

  • ✓ You want ticketing, shared inbox and chat in one support workspace.
  • ✓ You need AI to draft replies, summarize, tag and route customer cases.
  • ✓ Your support team benefits from rules, custom fields and collaborative ticket ownership.
  • ✓ A 14-day no-card trial is enough to test a representative workload before paying.

Check another tool when…

  • • Your priority is sales CRM or marketing automation rather than customer support.
  • • You need a permanent free support tier.
  • • Your agent count makes per-seat pricing too expensive.
  • • Your AI resolution volume would frequently trigger extra resolution packs.

1. Forward a real support inbox and check assignment, statuses and priorities.
2. Build the ticketing rules your team would actually need.
3. Test AI reply suggestions, summaries and tag recommendations on representative tickets.
4. Estimate monthly AI resolutions so overage packs do not surprise you later.
