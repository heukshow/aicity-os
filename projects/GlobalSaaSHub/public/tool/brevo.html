Brevo pricing & buyer guide

Official Brevo pricing and affiliate pages checked September 14, 2026.

Brevo combines email, SMS, WhatsApp, automation and CRM tools. Its free plan is a low-risk starting point for small lists and early campaigns.

Free
$0/month

300 daily email sends, up to 100,000 stored contacts, no credit card required.

Starter
From $9/month

Removes the free-plan daily send cap and scales by monthly email volume.

Standard
From $18/month

Adds broader automation, A/B testing, reporting and landing-page capabilities.

Who the free plan fits

  • You want to test campaigns without entering a card.
  • You can work within the 300-emails-per-day free sending limit.
  • You want email plus SMS/transactional messaging in one account.
  • You are not yet ready to pay for advanced automation and analytics.
