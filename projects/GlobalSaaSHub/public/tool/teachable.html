What the trial choice means

Teachable's public pricing page lists a shorter standard trial, while the 30-day option above provides more time to test course setup, checkout flow and product delivery before deciding whether a paid plan fits.

Use the extra trial time to test
  • Course or digital-product setup
  • Checkout and payment flow
  • Student access and delivery
  • Whether Starter's transaction fee still makes sense at your expected sales volume
Check before paying
  • Current product limits on your chosen plan
  • Platform and payment-processing fees
  • Monthly versus annual billing commitment
  • The exact trial and checkout terms displayed at signup
