Creatify review & buyer guide

Creatify focuses on ad creative: AI video ads, image ads, actors, templates and workflows designed to turn a product or brief into campaign-ready creative. The free plan gives a practical way to test output quality before choosing a paid plan.

Start with the free credits

Creatify's official pricing and help material currently says free accounts receive 10 credits each month. That is enough for a small output-quality test before you commit to a paid workflow.

Who Creatify fits

  • Teams testing AI-generated video ads from product inputs.
  • Marketers who also want image-ad generation in the same platform.
  • Brands that want AI actors and reusable ad templates.
  • Buyers who prefer a recurring free-credit entry point before paying.
