Why the free plan matters

The current Free tier includes up to 10,000 monthly pageviews, one domain, unlimited campaigns and all features. That is enough for a small site to test targeting, campaign design and experimentation without starting a paid subscription.

Where OptiMonk fits

  • Popup and embedded onsite campaigns.
  • Behavioral targeting and personalization.
  • A/B and conversion experimentation workflows.
  • Ecommerce-oriented conversion optimization.
  • Teams that want to test the full feature set before paying.

OptiMonk or Popupsmart?

Both target onsite conversion and both currently offer free entry, so the comparison is materially useful. OptiMonk emphasizes a full-feature traffic-capped free tier, while Popupsmart is a lighter popup-first option.
