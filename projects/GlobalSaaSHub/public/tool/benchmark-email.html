Benchmark Email pricing & buyer guide

Official Benchmark Email pricing, knowledge-base and affiliate pages checked September 14, 2026.

Benchmark Email is aimed at small businesses that want email campaigns, forms, reports and lightweight automation without a complicated starting setup. Its permanent free tier lets a small list test the workflow before paying.

Free
$0/mo

Up to 500 contacts and one user. Benchmark says no credit card is required to start.

Pro

Who Benchmark Email fits

  • You have a small list and want to test email marketing without entering a card.
  • You need an email builder, contact management, signup forms and reporting in one product.
  • You want a simple upgrade path rather than a large enterprise stack.
  • You are comfortable verifying the live free-send cap before committing your workflow to it.
