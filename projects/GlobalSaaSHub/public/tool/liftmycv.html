How LiftmyCV pricing works

LiftmyCV uses credits called Lifts. A standard Chrome-extension auto-apply currently costs 1 Lift and includes automated job discovery, application autofill, auto-apply and AI matching. More compute-heavy actions use additional Lifts.

Standard auto-apply
1 Lift
Auto-apply + resume AI

AI Matches auto-apply
3 Lifts
Job-specific resume generation
10 Lifts

Some features require an active subscription, including First Apply, Stealth Apply and Auto-Apply History. LiftmyCV also offers an Unlimited plan. Plan prices and allowances can change, so confirm the live checkout before purchasing.

Reasons to consider it

  • Combines matching, resumes, cover letters, autofill and tracking in one workflow.
  • Credit-based usage can make costs easier to connect to completed actions.
  • Free test is available without a card according to the current official site.
  • Useful for people applying repeatedly across supported job boards and ATS platforms.

What to check first

  • Automation does not remove the need to review role fit and application quality.
  • Some workflows and history features require a subscription.
  • CAPTCHAs or security restrictions can prevent automated submission on some sites.
  • Compare current Lift allowances and plan limits against your expected application volume.

Bottom line

LiftmyCV is most compelling for high-volume job seekers who want an integrated AI workflow rather than separate tools for matching, resumes, cover letters and application tracking. Start with the free test, check how many Lifts your preferred workflow consumes, then decide whether pay-as-you-go, Basic or Unlimited fits your application volume.
