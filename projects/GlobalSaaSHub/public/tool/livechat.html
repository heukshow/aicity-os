Product details

Overview

LiveChat combines website chat, sales campaigns, visitor tracking, conversation history and reporting in one support workspace. Its official pricing page offers a 14-day trial without a credit card.

Key Features & Capabilities

✓ Website live chat for support and sales
✓ Visitor tracking and recurring campaigns
✓ Chat history and performance reporting
✓ AI-assisted text intelligence

👍 Key Advantages (Pros)

  • 14-day trial with no credit card required
  • Campaigns and visitor tracking connect support activity to sales conversations
  • Team and Business add longer history and deeper reporting

⚠️ Considerations (Cons)

  • Published prices are per person and require annual billing
  • Enterprise pricing and some advanced capabilities require a sales conversation

Which plan should you test?

Start with Starter if one user is enough

Starter is the lowest-cost test path when one person will handle chat. Use the trial to confirm widget setup, visitor conversations and whether the 60-day chat history is enough.

Test Team if support is shared

Team is the more relevant test for multi-agent support because it adds unlimited users, unlimited campaigns, unlimited chat history and basic reporting. Compare the extra seat cost against the collaboration features you actually need.

LiveChat pricing FAQ

How much does LiveChat cost?

Starter is $19, Team is $49 and Business is $79 per person per month on annual billing. Enterprise uses custom pricing.

Does LiveChat have a free trial?

Yes. The official pricing page offers a 14-day free trial without a credit card.

Which plan fits a support team?

Team adds unlimited campaigns, chat history and basic reporting. Business adds scheduling, staffing prediction and advanced reporting for larger departments.
