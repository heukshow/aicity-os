Omnisend Review & Pricing 2026

Omnisend is built for ecommerce email automation. The practical choice is whether the permanent Free plan covers your current list or whether higher sending limits, Pro features and the current starter discount justify upgrading.

Free
$0

Send up to 500 emails per month to a maximum of 250 contacts. No credit card required.

Standard
From $16/mo

For growing stores that need more email volume. Paid pricing scales with billable contacts.

Pro
From $59/mo

Unlimited email. For newer subscriptions, SMS access is on Pro as a separately priced add-on.

Starter offer

30% off the first three months when paid upfront

Omnisend currently documents a 30% starter discount for a first-time paid subscriber who pays three months upfront. At the entry prices, the published examples are Standard at $11.20/month and Pro at $41.30/month for those first three months; regular billing resumes afterward.

When Free is enough

  • You want to test Omnisend before paying.
  • Your sending audience can stay within 250 contacts.
  • 500 emails per month covers your current campaigns.
  • Email, forms and basic automation are the immediate priority.

When paid plans make more sense

  • Your billable audience or monthly email volume exceeds the Free limits.
  • You need more predictable campaign capacity as the store grows.
  • You need current Pro-only SMS access for a newer subscription.
  • The three-month starter discount lowers the cost of a real launch test.
Pricing watch-out

Your real bill depends on billable contacts

The $16 and $59 figures are starting prices. Omnisend adjusts paid pricing by billable-contact tier, so use the live pricing calculator with your actual audience before upgrading. Standard email credits are also tied to billable contacts, while Pro includes unlimited email.

SMS rules changed in 2026: for paid subscriptions started on or after May 4, 2026, SMS is available on Pro as a separate volume-priced add-on. Free and Standard do not include current-plan SMS access for those newer subscriptions.
