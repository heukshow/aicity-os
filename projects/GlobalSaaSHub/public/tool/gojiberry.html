Who should skip it?

Gojiberry makes sense if…

  • • You sell B2B and care about buying signals.
  • • You want lead sourcing plus outreach automation.
  • • You need email/social coordination rather than email alone.
  • • $99/month is justified if the workflow replaces multiple prospecting tools.

Check alternatives if…

  • • You only need basic newsletter or bulk cold-email sending.
  • • Your outbound motion does not use social channels or intent signals.
  • • You need a lower entry price more than an integrated workflow.
  • • You cannot validate LinkedIn/social automation against your own account policies and risk tolerance.
