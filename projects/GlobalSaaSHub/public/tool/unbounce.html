  • ✓ Your bottleneck is landing-page conversion for paid or campaign traffic.

14-day trial plan

Test one landing-page workflow before you choose a plan

Use the trial to answer a narrow buying question: can your team build, publish, measure and hand off leads from one representative campaign? Record what happened; do not treat a template preview or an unfinished page as a successful test.

  1. Day 1 · Define the test

    Choose one page and one conversion goal

    Write down the audience, offer, traffic source and the single action the page should support. Use the same brief when you review the finished page.

  2. Days 2–4 · Build

    Create a representative page

    Check whether the editor, mobile layout and publishing flow can reproduce the campaign you actually need. Note any workaround or missing control.

  3. Days 5–7 · Connect

    Verify the lead handoff

    Submit synthetic test data and confirm where it arrives. Check the specific native integration, Zapier workflow or webhook your production process would require. Do not enter real customer data for this evaluation.

  4. Days 8–10 · Compare

    Test one meaningful change

    Define one hypothesis before creating a variant. Record the change, allocation and observed result. A small or internal-only sample is a workflow check, not proof of conversion lift.

  5. Days 11–14 · Decide

    Match the workflow to a paid plan—or pause

    Compare the required users, traffic, domains, testing controls and integrations with the current plan limits. Continue only if the working handoff and expected use justify the subscription; pause if the trial cannot reproduce the workflow or the required plan exceeds the approved budget.

Continue when

  • • The representative page can be built and published.
  • • Synthetic leads reach the required destination.
  • • The needed testing controls fit the selected plan.
  • • A named owner can maintain the page and results.

Pause when

  • • A critical integration or handoff remains unverified.
  • • Trial limits prevent a representative workflow.
  • • Traffic is too low to support the intended testing method.
  • • The required paid plan does not fit the approved budget.
