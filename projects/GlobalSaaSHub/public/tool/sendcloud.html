Cost detail buyers miss

The label fee is not the shipping cost

Sendcloud describes the per-label amount as a processing fee for creating labels. Carrier shipping charges are separate. If you exceed the discounted label volume on a paid plan, Sendcloud says an additional €0.15 per label can apply on top of the normal processing fee.

Which Sendcloud plan should you test first?

Free

Best first stop for very low shipping volume when you can use Sendcloud's available shipping rates and do not need a direct courier contract.

Lite

Start here when you need your own carrier contract, a few integrations and basic shipping rules without paying for a larger automation stack.

Growth

Worth testing when post-purchase branding, more automation, Pack & Go, review requests and phone support matter to your operation.

Premium / Pro

Evaluate these only when shipment volume, advanced returns, integrations or operational scale justify the higher fixed subscription.

Before you pay: calculate the real monthly cost

  1. Choose the plan whose integrations, users and shipping-rule limits actually fit your store.
  2. Estimate how many labels you create in a typical month and add the plan's processing fee.
  3. Check carrier shipping rates separately; they are not the same thing as Sendcloud's label fee.
  4. If you use your own courier contract, confirm that the selected paid plan supports your workflow.
  5. Verify the final account price for your country, because Sendcloud publishes regional pricing changes.
Example mindset: a cheaper subscription is not automatically cheaper if your label volume and carrier arrangement make the total cost higher. Test with your real parcel mix during the trial.

Affiliate-program status and link safety

Sendcloud publicly operates a free PartnerStack affiliate program and says affiliates receive a unique tracking link, 100% commission on a referral's first paid month, last-click attribution and a 90-day cookie. COSHUMA keeps those program terms separate from buyer links: a dashboard, application page, homepage or guessed parameter is not treated as a revenue link.

Publishing or verifying a tracking route would not by itself prove a signup, sale, commission or payout.

Bottom line

Sendcloud is easiest to evaluate by shipment volume and workflow complexity. Start with Free if your parcel count is tiny, or use the 14-day trial when you need a paid-plan feature. Before subscribing, calculate subscription + label-processing fees + carrier charges and verify the country-specific price shown in your account.
