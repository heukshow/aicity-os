What to validate during the 14-day trial

Use the trial to validate popup and workflow creation speed, targeting, visitor identification, recommendations and the handoff to your email/SMS stack. Do not upgrade based only on feature count; confirm the workflow can produce measurable conversion value for your traffic.

Where Justuno fits

  • Popup and onsite lead-capture workflows.
  • Visitor identification and audience segmentation.
  • Targeting, personalization and testing.
  • AI product recommendations for ecommerce.
  • Brands that want more managed CRO capability than a basic popup builder.

Justuno or OptiMonk?

The clearest difference for a first test is the entry model: Justuno currently offers a 14-day Lite trial, while OptiMonk has a permanent $0 plan with a traffic cap. Compare both before starting a paid subscription.
