Buyer Decision Box

Choose Taskip if…

  • You run a freelance business or agency and want clients, projects, invoices and support in one system.
  • You want workflow automation plus a branded client portal without stitching together many separate apps.
  • You value predictable team limits: Startup Team lists 10 members, Growing Agency 25, and Agency Pro unlimited team members.

Look elsewhere if…

  • You only need one narrow function such as basic task tracking or a simple form builder.
  • Your workflow depends on a specialized ecosystem that Taskip does not integrate with directly.
  • You need to validate a complex migration before committing; use the trial and test the exact workflow first.
