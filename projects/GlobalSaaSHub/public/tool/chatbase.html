Chatbase is a stronger fit when...

  • You want an AI agent trained on your own support or product knowledge.
  • You need customer interactions across more than a basic website chat box.
  • You can measure monthly conversation volume and map it to message credits.
  • You want Helpdesk, Voice or Telephony and can justify Standard or above.

Be cautious when...

  • Your expected traffic is too high or unpredictable to estimate credit cost.
  • You only need a simple static FAQ and do not need AI actions or multichannel support.
  • Your workflow requires enterprise controls such as SSO, audit logs or special data handling; verify Enterprise terms first.
  • You have not yet tested answer accuracy on representative customer questions.

Quick answers before you start

Is there a permanent free tier?

There is a $0 Free plan, but Chatbase currently says free-plan AI agents are deleted after 14 days of inactivity. Treat it as a testing environment, not permanent storage.

Where do Helpdesk and Voice begin?

On the current pricing page, Standard is the first listed tier that includes Helpdesk, Voice, Telephony, outbound campaigns and API access.

Should I choose annual billing immediately?

Chatbase advertises 20% off yearly plans, but the safer sequence is to validate answer quality and message-credit consumption first. A lower annual rate does not help if the workflow is a poor fit.
