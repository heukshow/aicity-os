Typedesk Review & Pricing

Updated for 2026 buyer decisions

Overview

Typedesk is a cross-platform text expander and canned-response manager for people and teams that repeatedly type the same replies, notes, links or templates. It works through desktop apps and browser extensions, and its free plan makes it possible to test the core workflow before paying.

Buyer Decision Box

Low-risk test available
Free plan: free forever, unlimited templates and up to 50 uses per week for one user.
Paid entry: Premium starts at $5 per user/month when billed annually.
Best fit: support, sales and admin teams that reuse replies across many apps.
Risk control: 30-day money-back guarantee on paid subscriptions.

COSHUMA take: start with the free plan if you are unsure whether text expansion will save enough time. Move to Premium when you need unlimited usage, team sharing or shared templates.
