Use the free workspace to answer four questions

  • Can you build the funnel format you actually need?
  • Does mobile preview match your campaign experience?
  • Do the required integrations sit in the plan you can justify?

What changes when you pay

The current Personal tier includes paid publishing for up to 10K monthly funnel views, while higher tiers expand traffic, integrations, testing and personalization. ConvertFlow also states traffic overages can change billed usage, so verify the exact limit and billing selector on the day you upgrade.

Compare a dedicated landing-page CRO option

Unbounce is more focused on landing pages and CRO, while ConvertFlow spans quizzes, forms, popups and broader funnel logic. Compare product scope before choosing a subscription.
