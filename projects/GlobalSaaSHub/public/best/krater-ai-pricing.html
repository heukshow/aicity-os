Buyer Decision Box

When does Krater make financial sense?

Good fit to evaluate

  • • You currently pay for several separate AI tools and want one workspace.
  • • You use multiple model families rather than one provider only.
  • • You can estimate whether 1,500 or 4,000 monthly credits matches real usage.
  • • Connected apps, agents, scheduled tasks or content generation replace paid tools you already use.

Pause before paying if

  • • You mainly use one AI model and already have a cheaper direct subscription.
  • • You cannot estimate credit consumption for your normal workload.
  • • Your decision depends on a specific model, integration or feature that must be verified on the live plan page.
  • • You would buy Max only for theoretical capacity rather than measured usage.

A simple way to choose the tier

  1. 1. List the AI subscriptions or APIs Krater might replace.
  2. 2. Estimate one month's actual chat, research, image, video, voice and automation usage.
  3. 3. Start with the lowest tier whose credits and feature limits cover that measured workload.
  4. 4. Upgrade only after usage data shows the smaller tier is constraining real work.
