What the 10-credit test should prove

Do not use all 10 contacts on random searches. Define one narrow ideal-customer profile first, then use the free credits to test the exact segment you would eventually buy.

Check whether the role, seniority, industry, company size and geography filters return the kind of buyers you actually need.
