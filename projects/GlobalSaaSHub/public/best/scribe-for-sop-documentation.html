SOP documentation

Is Scribe a good fit for SOP documentation?

Yes—when the expensive part of your SOP process is repeatedly capturing screenshots and writing click-by-click instructions. Scribe records the workflow and generates the visual steps, so the buyer decision is mainly whether that saved documentation time is worth upgrading from the free plan.

Choose Scribe when

You need SOPs, internal procedures, onboarding instructions or customer how-to guides built from real workflows.

Stay free first when

You are still testing whether automatic browser capture actually reduces your documentation workload.

Upgrade when

Desktop capture, redaction, screenshot editing, branding, exports or shared-team workflows become operational requirements.

Current pricing anchors

Scribe's official April 2026 invoice documentation gives a Pro Personal monthly example of $35 per user, while Pro Team is $85/month for up to five users plus $17/month per additional user. Annual billing is also available. Treat these as current official reference points, not permanent prices.
