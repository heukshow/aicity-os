What to compare before paying

Document volume

Check whether the plan limits envelopes, signature requests, documents, users, templates or API requests. A low headline price can be less attractive if real usage triggers limits quickly.

Team and admin controls

Look at role management, reporting, reusable templates, branding, signer authentication and whether adding users changes the economics.

Integrations

The right eSignature tool should fit where documents already live. Switching between apps can erase the time savings expected from electronic signatures.

Compliance and procurement

If your organization has specific legal, identity, retention or procurement requirements, verify those directly with the vendor before switching platforms.
