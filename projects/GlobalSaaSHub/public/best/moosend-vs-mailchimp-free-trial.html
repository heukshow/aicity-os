Choose based on the test you actually need

Choose Moosend first if...

You want the longer no-card evaluation window and enough time to build a list segment, send a real campaign, test automation and inspect reporting before deciding whether to pay.

Check Mailchimp first if...

You specifically want Mailchimp's ecosystem or want to keep a small list on its basic Free plan after the trial. Verify current contact and send limits before relying on the free tier.

A better buying test than comparing feature counts

Use the trial to recreate one workflow that matters: import a representative list, build one campaign, set one automation, create one signup path, then compare reporting and editing friction. That gives you a stronger buying signal than choosing from a long feature checklist.

FAQ

Is Moosend's free trial longer than Mailchimp's?

Yes based on the current official offers checked on September 12, 2026: Moosend's trial is 30 days and Mailchimp's current Standard/Essentials trial is 14 days.

Which one can I start without a card?

Moosend's current registration page explicitly says no credit card is required to get started. Mailchimp currently advertises a risk-free no-card trial path as well, although its pricing page says adding payment information unlocks the plan's full send allowance during the trial.
