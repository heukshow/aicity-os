The licensing difference that matters

Free · $0

Personal use only

Kittl says Free does not include commercial rights. Exports are also limited to low-resolution PNG/JPG, while vector and high-resolution export require a paid plan.

Pro · currently $19/mo monthly

Commercial entry point

Kittl's Pro help page includes a commercial license, high-resolution PNG/JPG, PDF/SVG vector export, premium libraries, custom fonts and unlimited projects. Kittl states commercial use is covered up to 500,000 copies.

Pro is usually enough when…

  • ✓ You sell print-on-demand designs at normal small-business volumes.
  • ✓ You create client graphics, brand assets, social creative or packaging.
  • ✓ You need SVG/PDF vectors or high-resolution 300dpi exports.
  • ✓ You want premium templates, fonts and commercial-ready output without jumping to the highest tier.

Check Max when…

  • • Your expected commercial reproduction volume may exceed 500,000 copies.
  • • You need the strongest current Kittl license ceiling rather than simply more design features.
  • • Your AI and storage workload is already heavy enough that Pro or Expert limits are material.

AI-generated content has a separate Free-plan warning

Kittl's current AI usage rules say Pro, Expert and Max users can use AI-generated content commercially subject to Kittl's terms and licensing. On the Free plan, AI-generated images become part of Kittl's shared content library and cannot be sold commercially on their own. If your product depends on AI-generated artwork, treat this as another reason to verify the paid-plan license before publishing.

Kittl commercial-use FAQ

Can I sell designs made on Kittl Free?

Kittl's current documentation says the Free plan is for personal use only and does not include commercial rights. Use a commercial plan before selling or using the final design for business purposes.

Does Pro cover print-on-demand?

Kittl states Pro includes a commercial license for eligible designs up to 500,000 copies. Check the current licensing terms for your exact asset mix and intended use before publishing at scale.

What happens if I cancel later?

Kittl's licensing help says commercial designs created under a qualifying paid subscription can remain usable after the subscription ends, subject to its current post-subscription and licensing terms. Verify those terms for the specific design before relying on long-term rights.
