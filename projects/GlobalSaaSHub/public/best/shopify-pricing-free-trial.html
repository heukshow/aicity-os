Use the trial to check these first

  • Can you add products, variants and shipping rules without creating operational friction?
  • Does your preferred payment setup work in your country and currency?
  • Can the theme and checkout flow support the products you actually plan to sell?
  • Which apps are truly required before they add recurring software cost?

Do not choose by headline price alone

  • Monthly and annual billing prices differ.
  • Payment-processing and third-party transaction fees can affect total cost.
  • Apps, themes and additional services can raise the effective monthly spend.
  • Country-specific features and pricing can differ from the U.S. pricing page.
