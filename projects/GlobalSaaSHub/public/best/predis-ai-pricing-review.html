What the credits actually mean

Predis.ai currently charges generation credits by asset type. Its pricing page lists 15 credits per ad creative, social post or photoshoot image; 200 credits per 8 seconds of standard/UGC video; 5 credits per 10 seconds of faceless video; and 15 credits per slide for multi-slide posts. That means the headline credit allowance matters more than the raw plan price if video is your main workload.

Predis estimates Core's 1,300 monthly credits at roughly 86 images or 9 videos, Rise's 3,200 credits at roughly 213 images or 22 videos, and Enterprise+'s 10,000 credits at roughly 666 images or 71 videos. Treat those as vendor estimates, not guaranteed output counts, because your mix of assets and edits changes consumption.

Choose Core if…

  • You manage one brand and want to test AI-generated social images, short videos, carousels, captions and hashtags.
  • Ten connected social accounts/channels are enough.
  • You do not need Predis.ai's auto-posting feature and can publish manually or through another workflow.
  • Your expected generation volume fits inside 1,300 credits per month.

Choose Rise if…

  • You need auto-posting rather than only content generation.
  • You manage up to four brands or client accounts.
  • You need more generation capacity and competitor-analysis runs than Core provides.
  • You want a middle tier before paying for Enterprise+ scale.

Trial terms buyers should notice

Predis.ai says every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, but a card is required to begin it. The company states that the card is charged when day 7 ends, so cancel before the trial ends if you decide not to continue. Its pricing FAQ also says payments after the trial are non-refundable.

Bottom line

Do not pick a tier from the monthly price alone. Start with three numbers: how many brands you manage, whether you need auto-posting, and how many image/video credits your real publishing schedule will consume. Core is structurally aimed at one-brand creation, while Rise adds multi-brand capacity and auto-posting; Enterprise+ is built for substantially larger multi-brand volume.
