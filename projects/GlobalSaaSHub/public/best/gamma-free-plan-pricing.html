Stay on Free when…

You are still checking output quality

  • ✓ Ten slides per prompt is enough for your test project.
  • ✓ Gamma branding is acceptable while evaluating.
  • ✓ You mainly need to import a PDF/PPTX or export a deck.
  • ✓ You have not yet proven that Gamma saves meaningful editing time.
Consider paid when…

A paid feature removes a measured bottleneck

  • • You need to remove Gamma branding for client-facing work.
  • • You need larger generations, stronger image models or custom branding.
  • • Analytics, advanced sharing, domains or API access matter to the workflow.
  • • Your AI usage volume consistently exceeds what lower tiers support.
Buyer caution

Do not upgrade based on a feature list alone

Start with a real deliverable you already need: a sales deck, internal proposal, client report or lightweight content page. Time how long the first usable draft takes, then count the manual edits needed. A paid plan is easier to justify when branding removal, analytics, API access or higher AI usage directly reduces work you can measure.

Lowest-risk next step

Use the no-card Free plan first. Only move up after a specific paid capability solves a real limit in the work you actually produce.

Gamma plan FAQ

Is a credit card required to start Gamma?

No. Gamma's current pricing page states that no credit card is required.

Which paid plan first removes Gamma branding?

Gamma currently lists branding removal under Plus. Pro adds custom branding, analytics, sharing, domains and API access.

Does COSHUMA's Gamma link prove a signup or commission?

No. COSHUMA previously verified the customer-facing PartnerStack route, but a link visit is not evidence of a signup, paying customer, commission or payout. Those are separate partner-side events.
