Buyer Decision Box

Which Jotform plan should you choose?

Choose Starter first when

  • You are validating a form workflow.
  • 100 monthly submissions are enough.
  • Jotform branding is acceptable while testing.

Upgrade to Bronze/Silver/Gold when

  • You repeatedly hit form or submission limits.
  • You need more storage or signed-document capacity.
  • You need to remove Jotform branding.

Use AI Agents when

  • Visitors ask repetitive questions before submitting.
  • You want a conversational intake or support flow.
  • You need an agent trained on FAQs, documents or web content.

Skip the paid upgrade when

  • Your current limits are not causing real friction.
  • A simple form or spreadsheet already solves the problem.
  • You have not tested the workflow on Starter yet.
