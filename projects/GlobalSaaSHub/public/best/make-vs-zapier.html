Make vs Zapier: which is the better-value automation platform?

Make is the stronger fit when you want visual branching, a larger free usage allowance and lower published paid entry prices at the compared usage level. Zapier is the easier fit when you prefer its Zap workflow model and want Forms, Tables and its broader AI-orchestration toolkit under the same account. The usage units are different, so do not compare credits and tasks as if they were identical.

Pick one workflow you genuinely need—for example, capturing a form response, adding a row to a spreadsheet and sending a notification. Build the closest free version in each platform. Usage model matters more than the headline price

Make explains that a module action in a scenario generally counts as a credit. Zapier says workflow steps and external connector calls consume tasks, with some usage rates varying by action type. A multi-step workflow can therefore consume multiple units on either platform. For a fair comparison, price the exact workflow and monthly run count you expect rather than comparing 1,000 Make credits with 100 Zapier tasks directly.
