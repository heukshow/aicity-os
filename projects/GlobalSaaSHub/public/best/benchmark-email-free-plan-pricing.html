Benchmark Email Free Plan: what you actually get

Evidence checked September 14, 2026.

The low-risk reason to try Benchmark Email is straightforward: the free plan costs $0, supports up to 500 contacts and Benchmark's help center says you can start without a credit card. That is enough to test the editor, contact workflow, signup forms and basic automation before paying.

Free-plan ceiling
500 contacts

One user. No card required. Good for validating a small-list workflow before upgrading.

Public-source discrepancy
2,500 vs 3,500 sends

The current English homepage shows 2,500 free sends/month; Benchmark's Jan 21, 2026 KB says 3,500. Treat the live account/region limit as authoritative.

When to stay free

  • Your active contact list remains at or below 500.
  • You are still testing deliverability, templates, forms and a basic automated sequence.
  • You do not need additional users or a larger send allowance yet.

When Pro becomes worth checking

Benchmark's current US pricing page lists Pro from $19/month on monthly billing, with annual billing showing a lower effective monthly rate. Upgrade only when contact volume, sending volume or paid-plan capabilities justify the recurring cost; verify the live checkout before buying.
