Buyer Decision Box

Which plan should you test during the trial?

Starter — $29/mo

Best for a small first test. Current limits include 5 pages, 1 user, 1 root domain and up to 500 monthly visitors.

Build — $99/mo

Best default for active campaign production. Unbounce currently lists unlimited landing pages and up to 20,000 monthly visitors.

Experiment — $149/mo

Choose this when A/B testing is the reason you are buying. Unbounce currently includes unlimited A/B testing and up to 30,000 monthly visitors.

Optimize is currently $249/month for higher-volume optimization needs. Concierge and Agency use custom pricing. Annual billing is separately discounted on Unbounce's public pricing page; verify the final checkout before paying.

Lowest-risk path

Use the 14 days to answer three questions before you pay

1. Can you publish fast enough?

Build one real landing page, connect your form and publish it. Do not judge the platform only from templates.

2. Do you need testing?

If A/B testing is central to your workflow, test Experiment rather than assuming Starter or Build will cover the requirement.

3. Which billing route fits?

Stay monthly for flexibility, or compare annual economics if you already expect year-round use and the partner annual offer is confirmed at checkout.
