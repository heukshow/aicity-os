AI tools for earning

Which AI tools can you use to make money?

Start with the way you want to earn: get leads, create content, sell online, automate work for clients, or grow traffic. Then compare the tools that can support that workflow.

Get leads Create content Sell online Automate work Grow traffic

COSHUMA does not promise income. Results depend on the offer, audience, execution, pricing and market. We focus on what each tool can practically help you do.

1 · Get leads

Use AI tools to find prospects and capture enquiries

This path fits agencies, consultants, freelancers and B2B sellers. The useful question is not “Which CRM is popular?” but “Which tool helps me find, capture and follow up with people who may buy?”

GoHighLevel

CRM, funnels and follow-up workflows for agencies and service businesses.

 Gojiberry

Find high-intent LinkedIn leads and automate personalised outreach.

 Fillout

Capture leads, applications and payments with a form-first workflow.
2 · Create content

Use AI tools to produce content you can publish, market or sell around

AI does not create revenue by itself. It can reduce the time needed to make videos, copy, audio and reusable content assets that support a channel, offer or client service.

Pictory

Turn scripts and long-form material into marketing and social videos.

 Descript

Edit audio and video like a document for podcasts, clips and client content.

 Writesonic

Create marketing copy and long-form content for campaigns and websites.
3 · Sell online

Use AI and SaaS tools to turn an offer into a checkout path

For products, digital goods or services, the monetisation path needs a storefront or funnel, a landing page and a way to capture or convert demand.

Shopify

Build and run an online store with checkout, products and commerce workflows.

 Unbounce

Create conversion-focused landing pages for offers and campaigns.

 ClickFunnels

Build sales funnels that move visitors from offer to checkout.
4 · Automate work

Use AI automation to deliver more work without doing every step manually

This path fits automation services, internal operations and client workflows. The economic value usually comes from saving time, handling more volume or packaging a repeatable service.

Make.com

Connect apps and build multi-step no-code workflows.

 Taskade

Combine AI agents, automations and collaborative workflows.

 Relevance AI

Build and deploy AI agents for repeatable business workflows.
5 · Grow traffic

Use AI tools to create demand before trying to convert it

Traffic is not revenue, but it is often the input. SEO, content optimisation and brand visibility tools can help you identify what people search for and improve the pages or content that answer that demand.

Frase

Research and optimise search-focused content around buyer questions.

 Brand24

Monitor brand mentions, competitors and visibility signals.

 GetGenie

Create and optimise search-oriented content with AI assistance.

The simple rule

Do not buy an AI tool because it is popular. Pick the money-making path first, then choose the tool that removes the biggest bottleneck in that path. Start with a free plan or trial when available, validate the workflow, and only then decide whether the paid plan is justified.
