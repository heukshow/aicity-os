Best CRM for Marketing Agencies in 2026

The right CRM depends on how your agency actually works. HighLevel is the strongest fit when you manage multiple client accounts and want CRM, funnels, booking and automation in one system. Pipedrive is simpler when the job is mainly sales pipeline management. HubSpot makes more sense when your team wants a broader sales ecosystem and is comfortable with seat-based pricing.

#1 · Best for agencies managing multiple clients

HighLevel

HighLevel's current agency plans are Starter at $97/month, Unlimited at $297/month and Agency Pro at $497/month. Starter supports up to three sub-accounts. Unlimited removes that cap, which is the main reason it becomes more attractive as an agency adds clients. Agency Pro adds SaaS Mode, advanced API access and markup/rebilling features for agencies that want to resell software-style services.

Best for
Marketing agencies, local-service agencies, lead-generation businesses and teams managing multiple client accounts
Watch for
Messaging, phone, AI and other metered services can create usage charges beyond the base subscription

#2 · Best for a clean sales pipeline

Pipedrive

Pipedrive currently offers a 14-day trial with no credit card required. Annual pricing starts at $14 per seat/month for Lite, $39 for Growth and $59 for Premium. It is a better fit than an all-in-one agency suite when your main need is tracking leads, deals, activities and follow-up rather than running client sub-accounts, funnels and marketing operations from the same platform.

Best for
Small sales teams, consultants and agencies that already have separate marketing tools
Watch for
Pricing is per seat, and features such as LeadBooster can involve additional cost depending on plan
View Pipedrive Pricing → Read the Pipedrive buyer guide
#3 · Best for teams already moving toward HubSpot

HubSpot Sales Hub

HubSpot's current Sales Hub page shows Starter from $7 per seat/month on annual billing and $20 on monthly billing. Professional starts at $90 per seat/month annually or $100 monthly, with a required $1,500 onboarding fee. HubSpot is the better comparison when your agency values a broad CRM ecosystem, deeper sales tooling and a path toward larger-team operations more than client sub-account economics.

Best for
Growing sales organizations, teams already using HubSpot products and businesses that want a broad CRM ecosystem
Watch for
Per-seat costs and required onboarding fees on higher tiers can materially change the total cost

How to choose quickly

Choose HighLevel when the number of client accounts is central to your business model and you want sales and marketing tools under one roof.

Choose Pipedrive when you mainly need a clean deal pipeline and prefer to keep marketing tools separate.

Choose HubSpot when you want a broader CRM ecosystem and expect to scale into more formal sales operations.
