GetGenie Free vs Paid: when should you actually upgrade?

GetGenie's Free plan is unusually useful for testing the full content-and-SEO workflow before paying. The key decision is not whether paid plans have more features; it is whether your real publishing workload repeatedly hits the free word, SERP-analysis or keyword-research limits.

Quick answer

Stay on Free

Best for testing quality, occasional articles and light SEO research. No credit card is currently required.

Choose Starter

Best first paid step when 2,500 AI words or the free SEO-analysis quotas are too restrictive but publishing volume is still modest.

Choose Writer+

Better for regular publishing, heavier SERP research and teams that repeatedly outgrow Starter rather than occasionally touching its limits.

Why Free is worth using first

  • No credit card is currently required.
  • 2,500 AI words per month lets you test output quality before paying.
  • Five SERP analyses and limited keyword research are enough to test one real SEO workflow.
  • There is no need to pay just to discover whether the WordPress/content workflow fits you.

The upgrade trigger to watch

  • Do not upgrade because a feature list looks longer.
  • Upgrade when limits interrupt work you would otherwise complete.
  • Starter is the lowest-risk paid step; Writer makes more sense when publishing is routine.
  • Pro or Agency should be justified by measured volume, not anticipated volume.

Lowest-risk buying path

Run one representative article from keyword research through SERP analysis, drafting and optimization on Free. If a real limit blocks the workflow, compare Starter against Writer using the live pricing page.
