Time2book is a stronger fit when

  • ✓ Your business sells classes, appointments, memberships, packages or drop-ins.
  • ✓ You want bookings, client records and payments in one system.
  • ✓ Your team needs attendance, reminders, waitlists, gift cards or coupon codes.
  • ✓ A no-card trial lets you validate the workflow before migrating customers.

Look elsewhere when

  • ✓ You only need a basic scheduling link and no studio operations.
  • ✓ A 0.9% platform fee materially damages your margins.
  • ✓ A required local payment method or integration is missing.
  • ✓ You need enterprise POS or membership features outside the product's current scope.

Use the 7 days as a decision test

1. Publish

Create one real class or appointment and publish the booking page.

2. Book

Complete a customer-style booking and inspect the confirmation flow.

3. Pay

Test the membership, package or payment setup that matches your real offer.

4. Decide

Only upgrade if the workflow saves more time or leakage than the subscription and payment costs.
