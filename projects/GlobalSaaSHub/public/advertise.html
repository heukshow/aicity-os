Campaign measurement

COSHUMA has prepared site-side measurement for sponsored inventory so campaign visibility can be evaluated without confusing an ad click with a sale.

Sponsored impressionsCounts when an active sponsored placement is actually rendered on a COSHUMA page.
Outbound sponsored clicksCounts clicks from an active sponsored placement to the advertiser's destination URL.

COSHUMA-side impressions and clicks do not prove advertiser signups, paid customers, sales or revenue. Downstream conversions remain separate advertiser/vendor evidence.

What makes a placement eligible?

The product should be relevant to the page and useful to the audience reading it. COSHUMA may decline a product that is misleading, unsafe, unrelated to the page or unsuitable for the site.

Payment buys the agreed advertising placement. It does not buy a favorable review, a higher editorial score, or an organic #1 ranking.
  • Every paid placement is labeled Sponsored or equivalent.
  • No paid placement changes COSHUMA's independent editorial rating or organic ranking.
  • No promise of clicks, leads, signups, sales or revenue.
