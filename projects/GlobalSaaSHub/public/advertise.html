Prepare copy for your placement

All three placements use a headline, a short description and one linked button. No logo, banner image or video is required.

Download the advertiser guide (PDF) · Download the application template

01 · Tool Page Sponsored

Offer a relevant next step

Your card appears after the main tool introduction and action buttons. Explain who your product helps and one relevant use case. Link to the product or offer described.

02 · Buyer-Intent Featured

Match the guide's task

Your card appears after the buyer guide's opening section. State the task your product helps with and a meaningful limitation or requirement. Keep the destination consistent with that task.

03 · Comparison Premium

Explain the alternative

Your card appears near the comparison's final decision section. State the situation in which a buyer should consider your product. Avoid unsupported superiority or savings claims.

Copy limits: headline 5–80 characters; description 20–240; button text 2–30. Use an HTTPS destination you are authorized to promote. Do not include passwords, payment details, confidential customer information or claims you cannot substantiate.

What happens next

  1. Application and quote. Choose an available page and send your copy. A saved application receives a unique reference. An email draft becomes an inquiry only after you send it.
  2. Payment confirmation. Pay only against the issued order when checkout is available. Company and tool names help identify the request; the unique order reference and exact amount are used to match the payment.
  3. Review and activation. Payment, copy approval, availability and the agreed dates must all be confirmed before your card is shown. Your purchased period starts when the placement actually goes live.
  4. End or extension. The card stops at the agreed end time. Extensions require a new confirmed period. See cancellation and refund terms.
