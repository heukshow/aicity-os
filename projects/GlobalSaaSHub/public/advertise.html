Fixed means one campaign in that position.

No rotation. This is exclusivity for the named position, not for the page, category or entire site. It does not guarantee that every visitor sees the ad.

Rotation shares the position, not the results.

The default interval is eight seconds while the unit is in view. Hover, keyboard focus, user pause, reduced-motion settings and inactive tabs can stop rotation. With three campaigns, a full uninterrupted cycle takes 24 seconds.

Check the format before booking.

A fixed booking is not silently converted into shared rotation. The number of creatives, dates, reporting rules and final price must be published before payment. No reach, click or one-third-impression guarantee is made.

The starting slide is selected randomly on each page load; eligible slides take equal-length turns. This is not a guarantee of equal impressions across visitors. Only the visible creative can earn a measured impression. New fixed and rotating positions do not yet have final prices. They remain unavailable until a fixed-price catalogue is published; historical text-ad rates are not rotating-ad prices. Paid image bookings remain closed.

Clear answers before you decide

What your placement would include.

What is COSHUMA?

An independent AI and software discovery site with tool profiles, buying guides and comparisons. COSHUMA is not the developer of the listed tools and does not sell their subscriptions.

Can I apply or pay now?

Yes for F1–F3. Submit the application and required PNG files first. Payment is not offered until COSHUMA approves the materials and reserves the selected position. F4–F5 and R1–R3 remain preview-only.

Do I need a consultation or custom quote?

No. The intended model uses published prices, availability and specifications. You choose your position or package. Materials are reviewed for compliance and relevance, not as a sales consultation.

How much reach is included?

No minimum reach, clicks, leads or sales are promised. Verified page-level audience figures are not supplied here. Sitewide visits and social followers are not ad impressions. Evidence and limitations must be shown before paid sales open.

Does the price buy a favorable review?

No. A future paid placement is a labeled advertising card, not an editorial endorsement. It does not change product ratings, rankings or recommendations.

What does rotating mean?

One creative shares a position with up to two others. Only one is visible at a time. On the article, the default interval is eight seconds while rotation can run. Preview buttons on this page are manual. Equal exposure and a one-third impression share are not guaranteed.

Will my logo replace COSHUMA's logo in the ad?

Yes. Your submitted product logo identifies your advertisement. The COSHUMA logo remains in the site header. In these house examples, COSHUMA is both the site operator and the advertiser.

When would the paid period start?

Only after approved materials, confirmed payment and the agreed schedule result in actual publication. Dates, changes, reporting and cancellation rules must be shown before payment. There is no automatic renewal.
