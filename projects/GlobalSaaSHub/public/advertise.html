Before you inquire

The details that matter.

Can I book an image advertisement now?

Not yet. The images above show COSHUMA house advertisements. Inquiries can help establish fit and future availability; they do not reserve a slot or enable payment.

How many people will see my advertisement?

We do not promise a reach figure. Ask for the selected page's dated traffic summary, sources and measurement limitations before considering a booking. Sitewide visits are not advertisement impressions.

What would I need to provide?

A relevant product link first. If a placement can proceed, the proposed materials include a correctly sized static image, headline, short description, button text, destination and evidence for claims. Requirements and the final preview are confirmed separately.

Does advertising buy a better review?

No. Paid placement would be labeled Sponsored and would not change editorial ratings, rankings or recommendations. COSHUMA house advertisements are labeled Advertisement · COSHUMA.

Is artwork production included?

No bespoke design, translation, landing-page production or distribution on other channels is included in the planning rates. Any additional work and cost would require a separate agreement.

What happens after I email?

The next step is a discussion of product relevance, available formats, evidence and dates. Do not send a payment until you receive a confirmed order and payment instructions. No response deadline or launch date is guaranteed.
