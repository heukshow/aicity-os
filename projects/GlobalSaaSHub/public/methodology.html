Editorial methodology

How COSHUMA evaluates AI & SaaS tools

COSHUMA is built to help buyers compare software before they subscribe. We separate product research, affiliate relationships and paid sponsorship so commercial relationships do not automatically become editorial recommendations.

Evidence first

Pricing, trials, limits and product claims are checked against available vendor or other clearly identified source material.

Commercial labels

Affiliate destinations and sponsored placements are labeled separately from ordinary official links.

Buyer fit over hype

Guides focus on who a product is for, likely trade-offs, pricing context and alternatives rather than vendor marketing claims alone.

Evidence levels

LabelWhat it meansWhat it does not mean
Official-source checkedA claim was checked against an official vendor page, documentation or public terms available when reviewed.It does not guarantee the vendor will never change the information later.

SponsoredA vendor paid for an explicitly labeled promotional placement.Payment does not buy a favorable review, editorial score or organic ranking.

What we compare

  • • Pricing and billing model when reliable public information is available
  • • Free plan or trial terms, including important checkout conditions when known
  • • Core use cases and key product capabilities
  • • Buyer fit, limitations and meaningful alternatives
  • • Official or verified partner destination used by the CTA

How rankings and featured areas work

Organic editorial placement should reflect buyer usefulness, relevance and available evidence. A paid placement must be labeled as sponsored. Affiliate eligibility alone does not guarantee a higher organic position.

Some homepage or guide starting points may be curated rather than statistically ranked. COSHUMA avoids presenting a curated list as a measured popularity ranking unless supporting data exists.

How we handle changing prices and terms

Software pricing, trials, promotions and affiliate programs change frequently. COSHUMA keeps source-backed records where practical and updates pages when newer reliable evidence conflicts with older information. The vendor checkout or contract remains the final source for the price and terms you agree to.

Correction policy

If a vendor, reader or partner finds an outdated price, incorrect trial condition, broken destination or materially wrong product claim, COSHUMA can review the underlying evidence and correct the page. Commercial status does not prevent a correction.
