MailerLite vs Brevo: which free plan fits your sending pattern?

MailerLite gives a small-list monthly allowance plus a 14-day premium-feature test. Brevo allows a much larger stored contact base on Free but caps email sends at 300 per day.

MailerLite

Best for a small list with a monthly send budget

Free is currently $0/month for up to 250 subscribers and 2,500 monthly emails. New accounts also get 14 days of premium-feature access with no card required.

Quick decision

  • Choose MailerLite when your list is small and a monthly sending allowance is easier to manage.
  • Choose Brevo when you need to store a much larger contact base and can work within a daily send cap.
