Voibe vs Murf AI

Choose Voibe if you want to speak instead of type across Mac or Windows apps. Choose Murf AI if you need generated voiceovers for videos, ads, training, or presentations.

Voibe: best for dictation

Voibe can be downloaded without a card, but its dedicated trial help says the 7-day Pro Monthly or Annual trial starts through Lemon Squeezy with a card on file; there is no charge until day 8 and cancelling before then means no charge. Public pricing currently shows $7.50/month, $59/year, or $149 lifetime. Paid options carry a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Privacy decision: Apple Silicon Macs can use fully on-device dictation. Windows and Intel Macs use zero-retention cloud processing.

Murf AI: best for voiceovers

Murf AI is designed for synthetic voice generation rather than system-wide dictation. Its free workspace lets you test generation without a card, while paid Creator and Business plans add higher limits and production features.

Best fit: narration, ads, training videos, presentations, and reusable generated voices.
