Brand24 vs Mention: which social-listening route fits your team?

Both products monitor online conversations, but the current buying paths are different. Brand24 publishes self-serve pricing and a 14-day trial with no credit card required. Mention currently presents a Company-focused pricing page with demo and free-trial entry points, while some older self-serve plans are now legacy products.

Choose Brand24 if…

  • You want to see published pricing before talking to sales.
  • You want a clearly documented 14-day trial before subscribing.
  • You need a straightforward way to test keyword volume, alerts, sentiment and reporting on your own brand.

Choose Mention if…

  • You prefer to evaluate a Company-oriented monitoring package with a vendor conversation.
  • You want social/web monitoring, Boolean search, sentiment, competitive analysis and team collaboration in one evaluation.
  • You are comfortable confirming final pricing and trial terms directly with Mention before purchase.

Lowest-risk way to decide

Use the same real monitoring test in both products: one brand name, one competitor and one important category keyword. Compare coverage quality, noise, alert usefulness and reporting before paying. Do not choose based only on the number of sources or a single feature checkbox.
