Typedesk vs TextExpander: which text-expansion workflow fits you?

Typedesk is a strong fit when reusable replies, shared templates, browser/desktop coverage and team-support workflows are the priority. TextExpander is a strong fit when you want a long-established shared snippet system with granular library, analytics and administration controls. Both now offer permanent free starting points, so test your real repeated-text workload before upgrading.

Buyer decision box

The fastest way to choose

Choose Typedesk first when…
  • Your team repeatedly sends support, sales or operations replies across many apps.
  • You want unlimited templates on the free tier and can work within 50 uses per week while testing.
  • Paid-team features such as shared templates, AI integrations, webhooks and onboarding matter to your workflow.
Choose TextExpander first when…
  • You want a dedicated snippet-library system with dynamic content and structured team governance.
  • You can validate the workflow with five active snippets on the free tier before paying.
  • You expect to use shared groups, activity history, organization analytics or higher-tier admin/security controls.

Typedesk: test the reply workflow first

Typedesk's official pricing page says the Free plan includes unlimited templates, 50 uses per week, desktop applications and browser extensions for one user. Its pricing page also describes paid team sharing, AI integration, webhooks and higher-tier onboarding or enterprise controls. If your real bottleneck is repeated support or sales text, measure how often you reuse templates before paying.

TextExpander: validate with five active snippets

TextExpander's current pricing page says its Free plan is $0 forever with no credit card required, unlimited total snippets and five active snippets at a time.
