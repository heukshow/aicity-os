Scribe vs Supademo: choose by the output you need

The cleanest distinction is the finished asset. Scribe is centered on automatically generated step-by-step process documentation. Supademo is positioned around interactive product demonstrations and walkthroughs. Both can explain software workflows, but they solve different primary jobs.

Choose Scribe for SOPs

  • Repeatable internal processes and standard operating procedures.
  • Automatic screenshot-and-text capture from browser workflows.
  • Training and onboarding instructions that need a clear numbered sequence.
  • Pro features such as desktop capture, redaction, screenshot editing and branding when needed.

Choose Supademo for demos

  • Interactive product walkthroughs for prospects or customers.
  • Product-led onboarding and feature education.
  • Shareable demos where the viewer should actively step through the experience.
  • Teams that care more about an interactive presentation layer than a classic SOP document.
