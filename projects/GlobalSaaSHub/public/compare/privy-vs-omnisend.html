What to test before paying

Compare quotes using the same contact count, monthly email volume, SMS destination countries and store traffic. Then test a signup form, a welcome sequence and a cart-recovery flow. Review mobile display behavior and suppression rules before importing your full list.

Do I need to replace my email platform to use Privy?

Privy lists a separate Pop-ups & Displays product that can sync collected contacts to supported platforms. Confirm that your existing platform is supported before selecting it.
