Fireflies is the better first test when…

Your meetings cross languages, teams or revenue workflows

  • ✓ You need transcription across more than a handful of languages; Fireflies currently advertises 100+.
  • ✓ You want AskFred, AI Skills, action items and meeting intelligence around recorded conversations.
  • ✓ A growing team needs conversation intelligence or admin analytics on the Business tier.
  • ✓ You prefer a $0 starting point and want to validate meeting capture before paying.
