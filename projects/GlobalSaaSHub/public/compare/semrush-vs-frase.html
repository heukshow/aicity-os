What to test before paying

Use the same existing page as a trial task: identify the search problem, prepare an improved brief, revise the draft and review how it reaches your CMS. Separately check whether you need backlink research, technical audits, daily rank tracking or multi-site reporting. Do not buy two overlapping subscriptions before identifying the missing step.

Can Frase replace Semrush?

Frase may cover the content workflow you need, but do not assume every Semrush research dataset, backlink tool, technical audit or reporting limit is interchangeable. List your essential tasks and test those before switching.

Which is cheaper?

Frase has the lower current entry price. That does not make it the equivalent product: Semrush covers a much broader SEO research and monitoring scope. Compare the exact workflow you need rather than price alone.
