Five-minute buyer test

Give both tools the same brief

Use one real brief such as “create a 10-slide customer pitch from this outline.” Judge first-draft structure, time to edit, brand consistency, export quality and whether the result is actually reusable. If Gamma wins on speed and presentation quality, keep testing there. If the work immediately expands into social, video, print or broader brand production, Canva is likely the more complete workspace.
