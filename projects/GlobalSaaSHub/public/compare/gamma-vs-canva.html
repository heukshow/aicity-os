A ready-to-use Gamma vs Canva test brief

Pick the presentation you actually need, copy the brief, and use the same source notes in both tools. This is a suggested comparison exercise, not a published speed or quality benchmark. Use public or sample material for your first test.

Compare the finished file, not just the first draft

  1. Time to usable: record minutes from starting the brief to a version you would actually send, including corrections.
  2. Accuracy: check every number and factual claim against the source notes. Count unsupported statements that need removal.
  3. Editing: change a heading, replace an image, and reorder a slide. Note where manual work gets in the way.
  4. Handoff: open the file or share link your recipient needs. Check fonts, page breaks, image placement, and whether further editing is possible.
  5. Cost to repeat: note any limit or paid feature you encounter. Check the plan that covers your actual workload before upgrading.

Your decision: start with Gamma if your repeat task is turning notes into presentations and its output passes your handoff test. Start with Canva if the deck belongs to a wider set of social, video, and brand assets. If neither passes, keep the free plan and revise the workflow before spending.
