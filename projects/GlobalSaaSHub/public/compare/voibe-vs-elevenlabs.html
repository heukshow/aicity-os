Voibe vs ElevenLabs

These products are not direct substitutes. Voibe is for turning your own speech into text across desktop apps. ElevenLabs is for generating, cloning and transforming voices, plus speech-to-text and other audio workflows.

Choose Voibe for everyday dictation

Voibe's current public pricing is $7.50/month, $59/year or $149 lifetime. A 7-day free trial is advertised with no credit card required, and paid options carry a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Best fit: email, documents, chat, prompts and code. Apple Silicon Macs can use fully on-device dictation; Windows and Intel Macs use zero-retention cloud processing.
