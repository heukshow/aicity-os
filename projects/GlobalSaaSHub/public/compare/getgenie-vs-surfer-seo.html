Which one should you choose?

Choose GetGenie if…

You publish SEO content regularly but want to keep software cost low, you value a no-card free plan, or you want writing, keyword research and SERP analysis in one workflow before paying for a more specialized optimization platform.

Choose Surfer SEO if…

Your team already has a writing workflow and the bigger problem is consistent on-page optimization, internal linking and measuring brand visibility across AI-search environments.
