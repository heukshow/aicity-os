Chatbase vs Reply.io

Chatbase is built for customer-facing AI agents and support automation. Reply.io is built for outbound sales outreach across email and multiple channels. The right choice depends on which side of the customer journey you need to automate.

Choose Chatbase when…

Inbound support is consuming time

  • ✓ You want AI agents trained on your business knowledge.
  • ✓ You need to answer customer questions before escalating to a person.
  • ✓ You want support-oriented integrations, analytics and customer-facing automation.
Current entry point: Free at $0/month with 50 message credits/month. Hobby is $40/month and currently offers a 7-day trial.

Choose Reply.io when…

Outbound prospecting is the bottleneck

  • ✓ You need cold-email and sales-sequence automation.
  • ✓ You want multichannel outreach that can include LinkedIn, calls, SMS or WhatsApp workflows.
  • ✓ You are optimizing prospecting volume, deliverability and meetings rather than support resolution.
Current entry point: Reply's Email Volume pricing currently starts at $49/user/month billed annually. Multichannel currently starts at $89/user/month billed annually and advertises a 14-day trial.

Best low-risk test

For inbound support, start with Chatbase's free plan and train one narrow agent on a real FAQ or product knowledge set. For outbound selling, use Reply.io's trial and test one clearly defined prospect segment. Measure the workflow outcome you actually care about instead of comparing unrelated feature counts.
