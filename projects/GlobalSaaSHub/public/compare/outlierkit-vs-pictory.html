Lowest-risk buying path

Do not buy both until you prove the bottleneck

1 · Test research first

If you struggle to choose topics, use OutlierKit's 10-credit no-card trial. Check whether its outlier/keyword results give you better publishable ideas.

2 · Test production first

If you already have ideas but editing is slow, use Pictory's 14-day trial. Build one real video from your own source content.

3 · Combine only when earned

The two products can complement each other, but paying for both only makes sense when research and production are both persistent bottlenecks.
