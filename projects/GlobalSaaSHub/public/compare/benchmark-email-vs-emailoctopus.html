Benchmark Email vs EmailOctopus

Official pricing sources checked September 14, 2026.

Both let a small business start email marketing without paying first. The practical difference is free-tier scale: Benchmark Email currently caps the free list at 500 contacts, while EmailOctopus lists up to 2,500 subscribers and 10,000 emails per month on Starter.

Benchmark Email

Free: $0, up to 500 contacts, one user, no card required.

Paid: current US Pro pricing starts at $19/month on monthly billing.

Watch: its current English homepage and Jan 2026 KB disagree on the free monthly send cap, so verify the live account limit.

Which one is the lower-risk starting point?

Choose Benchmark Email first if…
  • Your list is small enough for the 500-contact free ceiling.
  • You want its editor, forms, reporting and lightweight automation in one starter workflow.
  • You are willing to verify the live send cap shown in your account.
Choose EmailOctopus first if…
  • You need materially more free subscriber capacity.
  • 10,000 free monthly sends are useful to your early-stage list.
  • You accept free-tier branding while validating the workflow.
