Tally vs Jotform

Tally is the stronger free-first choice when you mainly need forms and generous response capacity. Jotform is broader when forms are part of a larger workflow involving payments, e-signatures, automation or AI-assisted customer interactions.

Decision pointTallyJotform
Free usageUnlimited forms and submissions within fair-use guidelinesFree Starter plan with usage limits
Best fitLean form creation and data collectionForms plus broader workflow tooling
PaymentsStripe-powered payment formsPayment forms and broader commerce/workflow options
AI angleNot the core buying reasonSeparate AI Agents path for conversational customer interactions
Safest first stepUse the official free productTest Starter or the relevant verified partner path

Choose Tally when…

  • Your priority is free forms with generous usage.
  • You want a lightweight, fast editor.
  • You do not need a larger app/workflow suite.
