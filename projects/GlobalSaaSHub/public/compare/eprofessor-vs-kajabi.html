eProfessor vs Kajabi: choose assessment depth and lower entry cost, or a broader all-in-one creator stack

These platforms overlap on course creation, selling and branded learning experiences, but the buying logic is different. eProfessor starts far lower and puts exams, assessments and training workflows near the center. Kajabi costs more but bundles a much broader marketing stack: website, funnels, email, community, payments and creator-business automation.

Choose eProfessor if

You need strong exams, certification or employee training, want white-labeling from the entry plan, or want to validate a course business at a much lower platform price.

Buyer decision box

The simplest decision rule

Assessment-heavy?

Start with eProfessor. Build one real exam, learner flow and report during the 7-day trial before paying.

Marketing-heavy?

Start with Kajabi if replacing separate email, funnel, community and course tools is the main source of value.

Unsure?

Map the next 90 days of work. If most tasks are teaching/testing, compare eProfessor first. If most are audience growth and selling, compare Kajabi first.

Payment and selling differences

eProfessor documents Stripe and Paddle as its current payment-gateway options and positions its platform subscription as flat pricing without a platform-level revenue-share percentage. Kajabi provides Kajabi Payments and also supports external providers, while its current pricing page lists plan-specific Kajabi Payments rates and separate third-party provider fees where applicable. Buyers should compare the full payment path for their country rather than comparing subscription prices alone.

Best low-risk test: build one real workflow before choosing

For eProfessor, test the exact course, exam or training workflow you plan to run. For Kajabi, test whether the integrated marketing stack genuinely replaces enough separate tools to justify the higher price.
