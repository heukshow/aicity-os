CRM buying decision

EngageBay vs HubSpot

Official product and pricing evidence checked September 14, 2026.

Choose based on operating complexity. EngageBay packages CRM, email, landing pages and support into a compact all-in-one plan. HubSpot offers a broader ecosystem of specialized hubs and integrations that can scale further but requires closer attention to which hub, seat and contact limits you need.

EngageBay

Start here if: one affordable suite covering CRM, email and service is the priority.

Free-plan anchor: 250 contacts, 1,000 branded emails, CRM, helpdesk, live chat and one landing page.

HubSpot

Start here if: ecosystem depth, specialized sales/marketing/service hubs and a wide integration market matter more than a compact bundle.

Watch: pricing and limits depend on the selected products, seats and marketing-contact scale, so verify the exact configuration.

Lowest-risk test

Create the same small workflow in each free starting point: capture one lead, send one follow-up, move one deal and resolve one support request. Compare setup time and whether the data stays connected before considering a paid plan.
