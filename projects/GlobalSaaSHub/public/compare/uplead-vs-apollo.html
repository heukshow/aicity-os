UpLead vs Apollo

Choose UpLead when the main job is verified B2B contact data and direct dials. Choose Apollo when prospect data, sequences, enrichment, dialing and workflow automation need to live in one sales platform.

Buyer decision box

Start with the workflow difference, then compare price.

Pick UpLead when

You mainly need verified emails, mobile direct dials, CRM/CSV exports and a focused contact-data subscription. Its 7-day, 5-credit trial is a low-volume way to test one real ICP before committing.

Pick Apollo when

Your team wants prospecting data plus ongoing outbound execution in the same product. Apollo's official pricing page includes a free plan and describes sequencing, CRM integrations, dialing and automation across paid tiers.

Watch the UpLead trial billing step

UpLead's support guidance says full trial activation requires payment details and the selected paid plan can begin after Day 7 unless canceled. Treat the five credits as a focused data-quality test and check live checkout terms before submitting payment information.

Do not compare credit counts blindly

The products meter different workflows. UpLead describes one credit as unlocking one contact for download or CRM export with email and mobile direct dial access. Apollo's credits support a broader platform, so raw credit totals are not directly equivalent.

If data quality is the question, test UpLead before paying

Use the five trial credits on one narrow buyer segment. If the returned contacts justify a recurring data subscription, compare monthly versus annual pricing before you continue.
