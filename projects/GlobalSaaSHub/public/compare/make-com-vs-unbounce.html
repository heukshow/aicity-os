The practical answer: many teams use both

A common stack is Unbounce for the acquisition page and Make.com for what happens after the lead converts—for example, sending form data to a CRM, enriching a lead, alerting sales, or triggering an onboarding sequence. If that is your workflow, the tools are complementary rather than competing.
