CustomGPT.ai vs Notion AI

These tools overlap around AI knowledge work, but they are built for different jobs. CustomGPT.ai is for deploying grounded AI agents over your own data. Notion AI is for using AI inside a collaborative docs, database and project workspace.

GROUNDED AI AGENTS

Choose CustomGPT.ai when the AI itself is the product or service layer

  • ✓ Build custom agents grounded in websites, documents and private business knowledge.
  • ✓ Standard currently lists 10 agents, 1,000 queries/month and 5,000 documents per agent.
  • ✓ RAG API access is included on the Standard plan.
  • ✓ Premium raises usage limits and adds features such as website auto-sync and branding removal.

Standard: $99/mo monthly or $89/mo billed annually.
Premium: $499/mo monthly or $449/mo billed annually.

Fast decision: which one should you buy?

Customer-facing or embedded AI bot

Pick CustomGPT.ai when users need a dedicated AI agent trained on your approved source material rather than a general workspace assistant.

Docs + projects + AI in one workspace

Pick Notion AI when people already create, plan, meet and collaborate in Notion and AI should operate inside that shared workspace.

Need both?

They can be complementary: Notion can remain the internal workspace while a separate grounded agent handles a customer or employee knowledge experience.

Before you start a trial

CustomGPT.ai: estimate query volume, source-document volume and the number of agents you actually need. The jump from Standard to Premium is large, so validate the workflow during the trial first.
Notion AI: price the entire workspace by member count and check whether you need Business-level AI features or only the limited AI trial on Free/Plus.
