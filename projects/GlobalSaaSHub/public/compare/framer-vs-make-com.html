WEBSITE BUILDING vs WORKFLOW AUTOMATION

Framer vs Make.com

These products are not true substitutes. Framer is for designing, publishing and hosting websites. Make.com is for automating what happens between apps after a visitor, lead, order or internal event occurs.

Pricing and public product details checked September 4, 2026 against the vendors' official pages.

Choose Framer if

You need to launch the website itself

  • ✓ You need a visual website builder, CMS and managed hosting.
  • ✓ SEO, custom domains and a polished marketing site are the priority.
  • ✓ Designers and marketers need to publish without maintaining a separate frontend stack.
  • ✓ Your next bottleneck is the page experience, not cross-app automation.

The higher-value answer: many teams need both

A practical stack is Framer for the customer-facing site and Make.com for the workflow after conversion. They solve different parts of the funnel, which is why choosing only one can create the wrong trade-off.

Lead capture

Build the landing page in Framer, then use Make to route a form submission into a CRM, spreadsheet, email sequence or team alert.

Content operations

Publish the marketing site in Framer while Make moves approved data between content, analytics, support and internal systems.

Low-risk test

Use the free tiers first. Validate the page and the workflow separately before upgrading either product.

Already have a website? Automate the work behind it.

If your site is already live, Make.com's free tier is the lower-friction next test: connect one real lead or operations workflow, measure the time saved, then upgrade only if the automation proves useful.
