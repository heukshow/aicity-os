Webflow vs Unbounce

These products overlap around landing pages, but they solve different primary jobs. Webflow is a broader website, CMS and hosting platform. Unbounce is a focused landing-page and conversion-optimization platform for campaign traffic, testing and CRO.

Choose Webflow when
The website itself is the product surface

Best fit for a full marketing site, content-rich CMS, custom domain, site-wide SEO/AEO, design-system control and long-lived web experiences.

Choose Unbounce when
Paid traffic needs dedicated conversion pages

Best fit for campaign landing pages, fast iteration, native A/B testing, conversion reporting and AI-assisted traffic optimization.

Where Webflow wins

  • ✓ Full-site visual design, hosting and domain workflow in one platform.
  • ✓ CMS for content-rich sites on Premium and above.
  • ✓ Site-wide design systems, interactions and longer-lived web experiences.
  • ✓ Built-in AI features plus SEO/AEO tooling for the broader website.
  • ✓ Better fit when your landing pages must live inside a larger branded site architecture.

Where Unbounce wins

  • ✓ Dedicated campaign landing-page workflow for marketers and agencies.
  • ✓ Native A/B testing on Experiment and higher tiers.
  • ✓ Smart Traffic AI optimization on Optimize and higher tiers.
  • ✓ Campaign-focused traffic, conversion and variant controls instead of full-site complexity.
  • ✓ Unbounce MCP is included across plans for compatible AI-assisted landing-page workflows.
Decision rule

Do not buy Unbounce just to build a normal company website

If you need navigation, CMS content, broad site architecture and a durable brand website, start with Webflow. If the money is already being spent on ads or campaigns and the bottleneck is landing-page conversion, Unbounce becomes the more purpose-built tool. The higher Unbounce price is easier to justify when a measurable conversion lift matters more than general website breadth.
