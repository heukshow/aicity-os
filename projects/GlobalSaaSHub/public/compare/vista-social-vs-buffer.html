Vista Social vs Buffer: bundles for teams or pay per channel?

Buffer is easier to start: its free plan supports three channels, while paid plans begin at a listed $5 per channel/month billed annually. Vista Social begins at $79/month, but bundles 15 profiles, two users, broader engagement workflows and reporting. Your channel count—not the headline price alone—decides the value.

Choose Vista Social if

You manage many profiles, need shared inboxes, approvals, reporting, DM automation, client workflows or a path to white-label operations.

Vista Social becomes more compelling when the work includes comments and reviews, approval stages, client reporting, DM lead capture, social listening or many connected profiles. Professional includes 15 profiles; Advanced doubles that to 30 and adds stronger workflows.

BUFFER VALUE

Better when simplicity is the problem

Buffer's free plan is enough to test publishing, AI assistance, basic insights and community replies across three channels. Essentials removes scheduled-post limits and adds advanced analytics from $5 per channel/month on annual billing.

Per-channel pricing stays understandable, but it compounds as accounts grow. A client with several brands and profiles can cross into Vista Social's bundled-price territory quickly.

Vista Social vs Buffer FAQ

Which is cheaper for one person?

Buffer is usually cheaper for a small number of channels because it has a free plan and paid per-channel pricing. Vista Social becomes more competitive when you need many profiles or its bundled team and engagement features.

Which is better for an agency?

Choose Vista Social for deeper client approval, reporting, listening and white-label paths. Choose Buffer when lightweight scheduling and paying only for active channels matter more.

Do both have 14-day trials?

Yes. Both official pricing pages currently advertise 14-day trials for paid plans. Buffer also lets you start directly on its free plan.

What can unexpectedly increase cost?

Buffer charges by connected channel. Vista Social bundles profiles but separately lists paid add-ons such as broader listening, Employee Advocacy and X integration.
