Which one should you buy?

Castmagic is the clearer purchase when you already publish long-form audio/video and the expensive part is turning each recording into multiple finished assets. OutlierKit is the clearer purchase when your bigger risk is spending time producing YouTube videos around weak topics, keywords or niches.

They can also be complementary: research the opportunity in OutlierKit first, then repurpose the finished recording in Castmagic.
