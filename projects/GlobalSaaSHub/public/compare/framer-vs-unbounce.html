Framer vs Unbounce

Framer and Unbounce overlap around landing pages, but they solve different primary problems. Framer is a flexible visual website platform for polished marketing sites. Unbounce is purpose-built for campaign landing pages, A/B testing and conversion optimization.

Pricing and product details checked against official vendor pages on September 4, 2026.

Choose Framer when

Your website is the product surface

  • ✓ You need a polished multi-page marketing site, CMS and responsive visual design.
  • ✓ Your design team wants staging, branching and broader website publishing workflows.
  • ✓ You want a free plan to prototype before connecting a commercial custom domain.

Choose Unbounce when

Paid-campaign conversion is the KPI

  • ✓ You need dedicated landing pages without rebuilding your main website.
  • ✓ A/B testing, custom scripts, pixels, lead forms and campaign optimization matter more than a full-site design system.
  • ✓ You want to validate a real campaign with a 14-day free trial before paying.

Low-risk test path

Test Unbounce with one real campaign before committing

Use one existing paid campaign, rebuild its landing page, connect the real form or conversion goal, and compare conversion performance during the trial. If you do not have a campaign worth testing, Framer's free plan is the lower-pressure place to prototype a general website first.
