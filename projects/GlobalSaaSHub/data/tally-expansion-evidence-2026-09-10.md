# Tally expansion evidence — 2026-09-10

## Why this tool was added
- Tally expands COSHUMA's form-builder coverage and creates a same-micro-category comparison opportunity with Jotform.
- Repository search before the change did not find an existing COSHUMA Tally tool page.

## Official product evidence
- Official homepage: https://tally.so/
- Official pricing page: https://tally.so/pricing
- Tally states that unlimited forms and submissions are available free within fair-use guidelines.
- The live pricing page currently presents Pro and Business upgrade paths.
- Tally supports logic/calculations, file uploads, integrations and Stripe-powered payment forms according to its official product/help pages.

## Pricing conflict guardrail
- The current live pricing page and some official Tally help pages show different paid-plan amounts.
- COSHUMA therefore does not publish a single paid Tally price in the new comparison page until the vendor's official sources converge.

## Affiliate state
- A historical Tally blog post describes an affiliate program, but this run did not verify a current COSHUMA account-specific customer-facing tracking URL.
- Tally CTAs added in this change are official non-affiliate links only.
- Do not invent or infer a Tally referral parameter from historical program materials.

## Monetization behavior
- The Tally vs Jotform comparison may use only an already repository-verified Jotform customer-facing tracked URL for the Jotform side.
- No click, signup, customer, commission or revenue is claimed from this expansion.
