## Fresh first-party evidence checked

Voibe's public pages currently contain two statements that can be read differently:

1. The Voibe homepage and several marketing pages say **"Try Voibe for free" / "No credit card required"** and also advertise a **7-day free trial**.
2. Voibe's dedicated **Free trial** help article is more specific: downloading Voibe does not require a card, but starting the **7-day Pro Monthly or Pro Annual trial through Lemon Squeezy does require a card on file**. The card is not charged until day 8. Cancelling before day 8 means no charge.
3. The same official help article states every plan has a **30-day money-back guarantee**.

Primary sources rechecked:

- https://www.getvoibe.com/
- https://www.getvoibe.com/help/free-trial/
- https://www.getvoibe.com/for/privacy/

## Operational decision

Use the dedicated trial-help article as the authoritative source for the mechanics of the Pro trial because it directly explains the checkout flow.

COSHUMA must therefore not state that the 7-day Pro trial itself requires no card. Safe buyer wording is:

> Voibe can be downloaded without a card. Its dedicated trial help says the 7-day Pro Monthly/Annual trial is started through Lemon Squeezy with a card on file, but the card is not charged until day 8; cancel before then and there is no charge. Paid options are covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Keep pricing promotional language clearly time-sensitive. Current public pricing observed on 2026-09-12 is $7.50/month, $59/year and $149 lifetime, but those promotional amounts and lifetime availability can change.
