Choose Personal when

  • ✓ You are building one primary site
  • ✓ 25 pages and 200 blog posts are enough
  • ✓ You do not need a team of collaborators
  • ✓ You want the lowest recurring Dorik plan

Choose Business when

  • ✓ You need up to 3 custom domains
  • ✓ You want unlimited pages and blog posts
  • ✓ Up to 10 collaborators matters
  • ✓ You are building sites for a business or a small client portfolio
