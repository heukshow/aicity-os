Where Scribe is strongest

  • Capture browser workflows into visual step-by-step guides without manually taking every screenshot.
  • Use Pro for desktop capture, advanced screenshot editing, redaction, branding and broader export options.
  • Share a guide by link or embed it into an existing knowledge base or website.
  • Use Pro Team when several people need to collaborate in one shared documentation workspace.

Scribe or Supademo?

Choose Scribe when the main job is repeatable SOP and process documentation. Compare Supademo when the main job is an interactive product walkthrough for prospects, onboarding or product education. The overlap is real, but the buying intent is different.
