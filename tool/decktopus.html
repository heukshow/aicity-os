What you are actually buying

AI deck generation

Enter a topic or upload a file and Decktopus generates the presentation structure, copy and design together rather than starting from a blank slide.

Brand import

Paste a website URL and Decktopus can pull the brand colors, fonts and logo into the presentation workflow automatically.

PPT, PDF and PNG export

Paid plans support common presentation export formats, which matters if the final deck must leave the web app or be edited elsewhere.

Loop rehearsal

Decktopus also includes presentation practice tools that simulate audience questions and provide feedback on delivery, pacing and clarity.

Decktopus is a good fit when...

  • You regularly turn a topic, outline or document into a presentation.
  • You want AI to handle slide structure and visual direction, not just write text.
  • You need PowerPoint/PDF export and fast sharing.
  • Presentation rehearsal and audience-question practice are useful to you.

Think twice when...

  • You only need occasional slides and want a permanent free tier.
  • You prefer pixel-level manual design control over AI-generated structure.
  • Your workload is heavy enough that monthly AI credits may require top-ups.
  • Your organization needs enterprise governance that should be validated in a sales conversation first.

Bottom line

Decktopus is most compelling for buyers who value speed from idea to presentable deck. The low-risk entry path is to use the free signup credits first, then pay only if the generated slides, brand import and rehearsal workflow save enough time to justify the subscription.
