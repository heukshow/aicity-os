Lowest-risk test: use the 14-day trial with a representative contact sample and build one real automation. Check segmentation, reporting and CRM fit before importing the full database or paying for a larger contact tier.

Choose ActiveCampaign when…

  • ✓ Your workflows require branching and multi-step automation rather than basic newsletters.
  • ✓ Marketing and CRM signals need to drive the same customer journeys.
  • ✓ Segmentation and lifecycle messaging matter more than the cheapest entry price.
  • ✓ You want AI-assisted campaign and automation tools inside the same platform.

Compare alternatives when…

  • • You only send occasional newsletters and need the lowest possible monthly cost.
  • • Your contact database is large but automation requirements are simple.
  • • You need a dedicated outbound-sales sequencer rather than lifecycle marketing automation.
  • • You need a fully free long-term plan instead of a time-limited trial.
