What to validate during the free trial

Build at least one real lead-capture or product-promotion campaign, test mobile behavior and targeting, connect the email platform you actually use, and verify whether the workflow improves a measurable signup or sales step before paying.

Where Sleeknote fits

  • Email and lead-capture popups.
  • Product-promotion and ecommerce onsite campaigns.
  • Visitor guidance and personalized onsite messaging.
  • Gamification and audience segmentation workflows.
  • Teams that prefer a no-card evaluation before a visitor-based subscription.
