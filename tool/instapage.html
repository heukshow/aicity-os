The 14-day trial requires a card

Instapage's current pricing FAQ says a credit card is required to start the Create or Optimize trial. The selected plan can begin charging when the trial ends unless you cancel. The trial can also end early if its 2,500-visitor trial limit is reached, so check the billing screen before starting.

Where Instapage fits

  • Fast landing-page creation with reusable blocks and AI-assisted copy.
  • Visual collaboration for marketing teams and agencies.
  • Server-side A/B testing and dynamic text replacement on Optimize.
  • Enterprise personalization, heatmaps and advanced publishing on Convert.
