Use Free before any paid trial

Claspo's current paid-plan trial requires billing details and can convert to a paid subscription after the 14-day trial. COSHUMA therefore recommends the permanent Free plan for zero-risk evaluation and does not start the paid-plan trial on your behalf.

Claspo or Poptin?

Both have permanent free entry, but their limits use different units: Claspo currently publishes pageviews while Poptin publishes visitors. Those numbers should not be treated as equivalent.
