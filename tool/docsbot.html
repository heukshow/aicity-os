What to check before upgrading

  • • AI-credit use varies by model and action complexity
  • • The free bot has only 50 source pages and 100 monthly credits
  • • Paid-plan limits differ substantially in bots, pages, actions and users
  • • When monthly AI credits run out, the bot stops responding unless capacity is increased or resets
  • • Auto-add credits is optional and can increase paid spend, so enable it only deliberately
