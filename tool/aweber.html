Current AWeber pricing for up to 500 subscribers

The figures below are the starting prices shown in AWeber's current pricing documentation, rechecked on September 18, 2026. Monthly charges and annual totals are shown separately. Confirm the subscriber tier and billing term at checkout.

Lite
$15/month or $150/year

Monthly billing or the stated annual total, for up to 500 subscribers.

  • Up to 10× subscriber volume in monthly sends.
  • 1 email list.
  • 3 landing pages and 3 email automations.
  • Up to 3 users and 1 custom segment.
Plus
$30/month or $240/year

Monthly billing or the stated annual total, for up to 500 subscribers.

  • Up to 12× subscriber volume in monthly sends.
  • Unlimited email lists, landing pages and automations.
  • Unlimited custom segments and users.
  • Advanced reporting, analytics and priority support.
Trial: AWeber currently promotes a 14-day free trial. Its official trial documentation says a card is kept on file and the selected paid plan is charged automatically when the trial ends unless you cancel first.

What AWeber can replace in your stack

Email campaigns: newsletters, broadcasts, templates, RSS-to-email and HTML email creation.
Automation: autoresponders, email automations, tagging and behavioral automation capabilities depending on plan.
Lead capture: landing pages, signup forms, segmentation and subscriber-management tools.
Creator sales tools: ecommerce features, subscriptions, payment plans, sales tracking and purchase tagging are listed in the platform's plan comparison.

Choose AWeber if...

  • You want email marketing and basic sales tools in one platform.
  • You prefer a mature product with straightforward creator and small-business workflows.
  • You expect to use landing pages and automations without stitching together several services.
  • You want to test the workflow during a currently advertised 14-day trial before committing.

Check carefully before buying if...

  • You need more than the Lite plan's list, landing-page or automation limits.
  • Your subscriber count is growing quickly enough that higher-volume pricing materially changes the total cost.
  • You need advanced segmentation, analytics or branding removal but are comparing only the Lite price.
  • You are choosing between AWeber and a free-first competitor and total cost is your main priority.

For a small list, compare Lite against Plus based on the limits you will actually use, then confirm the live billing toggle and checkout total before committing.
