Know what to test before you click

Use the free trial to test a real support workflow instead of browsing features. Connect one knowledge source, run representative customer questions, test a human handoff, watch how many AI resolutions your workflow consumes, and compare the result with the Essential and Growth limits before choosing a paid plan.

1. Connect a real FAQ, help center, or website knowledge source.
2. Test the questions your customers actually ask.
3. Verify escalation and human takeover for edge cases.
4. Check AI-resolution usage before selecting a paid tier.
