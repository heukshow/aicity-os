Trial billing detail worth checking

Landingi says a valid credit card may sometimes be requested for the free trial. Its pricing FAQ also says the selected paid subscription can begin after the trial unless you cancel. Check the exact checkout terms shown to your account before starting.

Where Landingi is strongest

  • Dedicated landing-page production rather than a broad all-purpose website workflow.
  • Lunar AI and a classic visual builder for page creation.
  • Forms, integrations and basic analytics in the page workflow.
  • EventTracker, server-side A/B testing and optimization features on higher plans.
  • Programmatic pages, client sub-accounts and agency controls on higher tiers.

Landingi or Unbounce?

Both target landing-page buyers, so this is a practical comparison. Start with Landingi when its page-volume, AI-generation and agency workflow match your campaign operation. Compare Unbounce when its established CRO workflow, Smart Traffic and current offer are more relevant.
