Which Pictory plan makes sense?

Starter: simple solo production

Best when you mainly need script-to-video, automatic captions, stock media and straightforward branded videos without the higher-end avatar and voice-cloning tools.

Professional: heavier creator workflow

The stronger fit for frequent content production because the live plan adds substantially more video minutes and storage plus custom avatars, voice cloning, advanced AI tools and long-video summarization.

Team: shared production

Useful when multiple people need a shared workspace, much larger video quotas, more storage and brand kits, plus onboarding and team training.

✓ Turn ideas, scripts, URLs, documents, PDFs and PowerPoints into video

✓ Use integrated stock media from Getty Images and Storyblocks
✓ Use ElevenLabs AI voices, with allowances varying by plan
✓ Access custom avatars and voice cloning on Professional and above
✓ Create social clips and repurpose long-form video content

COSHUMA has not treated a generated sample as hands-on proof. Use one source you are allowed to publish—a script, URL, document or long-form recording—and note the corrections required at each checkpoint.

Choose OutlierKit or TubeBuddy if...
