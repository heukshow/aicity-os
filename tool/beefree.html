Design-first workflow

RGE Studio is the cleaner fit when…

  • ✓ Your team mainly needs to create responsive email and page designs quickly.
  • ✓ Approval, commenting, co-editing or reusable design systems matter.
  • ✓ You already have a sending platform and want a stronger design layer.

Another option to compare

AWeber is worth comparing when…

  • ✓ You need subscriber management, broadcasts and automated email sequences.
  • ✓ Landing pages, signup forms and campaign delivery should live in one account.
  • ✓ You want a small-business email platform rather than a standalone design-production layer.
  • ✓ Your buying decision depends on list size and monthly send capacity.
AWeber's current official pricing documentation lists a Free plan up to 500 subscribers, Lite from $15/month or $150/year, Plus from $30/month or $240/year, and Unlimited at $899/month. Its live pricing page also advertises a 14-day trial. Pricing scales with subscriber count.
