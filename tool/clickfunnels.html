Bottom line

Launch is the lowest-cost current ClickFunnels entry point for a single workspace. Scale becomes more attractive when you need multiple workspaces, larger contact/email limits and API/webhook access. Optimize is aimed at higher-volume marketers, while Dominate is an annual-only tier for much larger limits and onboarding. Start with the smallest tier that fits your actual contact, email and workspace needs rather than buying capacity you will not use.
