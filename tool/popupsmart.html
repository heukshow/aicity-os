Start with the free tier before paying

For a buyer who mainly needs to validate popup design, triggering, targeting and lead capture, the free tier is the lowest-risk starting point. Move to a paid tier only after measuring the traffic volume and campaign limits that matter to your site.

Where Popupsmart fits

  • Lead-capture popups and forms.
  • Discount, announcement and ecommerce onsite campaigns.
  • Audience targeting and behavior-based triggers.
  • Gamified campaign formats and conversion-focused messaging.
  • Campaign reporting and integrations for downstream marketing workflows.

Free plan or paid tier?

The useful buying question is not whether Popupsmart has more features than a generic marketing suite. It is whether your site needs enough popup volume, targeting depth and campaign scale to justify moving beyond the free plan.
