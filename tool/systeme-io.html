Who should start with the free plan?

The free tier is the lowest-risk option for a solo creator, early-stage business or marketer who wants to test funnels, email automation and course delivery before committing to a paid subscription. If your list is above 2,000 contacts or you need more funnels, courses or automated webinars, compare the paid limits before upgrading.
