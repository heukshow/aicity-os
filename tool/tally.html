Tally Forms review & buyer guide

Tally is a strong low-cost choice when you want fast, no-code forms without paying just to unlock basic logic or response capacity. When Tally is the better fit

  • You need unlimited free forms and submissions under fair-use guidelines.
  • You want a document-like form editor and quick setup.
  • You need conditional logic, signatures, file uploads, payments or integrations without starting on a paid tier.
  • You prefer a lightweight form tool over a broader workflow suite.

Compare before you choose

Jotform is broader across forms, e-signatures, workflows and AI-assisted customer interactions. Tally is the cleaner fit when low-cost form creation and generous free usage are the priority.
