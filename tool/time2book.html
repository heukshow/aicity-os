Choose Time2book when

  • ✓ You run personal training, yoga, Pilates, a gym, martial-arts academy or another class-based fitness business
  • ✓ You want booking, client records and payments in the same product
  • ✓ Memberships, packages, drop-ins, gift cards or coupon codes are part of your revenue model
  • ✓ You want a no-card trial before moving your live schedule

Look elsewhere when

  • ✓ You only need a basic calendar link and not studio operations
  • ✓ Your business needs a highly specialized POS or enterprise membership stack
  • ✓ The extra 0.9% platform fee materially hurts your unit economics
  • ✓ A required payment method or integration is not supported in your country
