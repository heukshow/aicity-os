Choose Framer when…

  • ✓ Design quality and speed matter more than a traditional developer workflow.
  • ✓ You need landing pages, marketing pages or a visual CMS site.
  • ✓ Your team can benefit from staging, previews and visual collaboration.
  • ✓ You want built-in hosting instead of maintaining a separate deployment stack.

Compare alternatives when…

  • • Your main need is conversion experimentation rather than general site design.
  • • You need a complex web application or backend-heavy product.
  • • Many editors make seat costs a major part of your budget.
  • • Your content model exceeds the CMS limits of the plan you are considering.
