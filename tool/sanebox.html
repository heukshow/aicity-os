Buying decision

Choose by the job you actually need done

Choose SaneBox when…

  • Your main problem is incoming-email overload.
  • You want SaneLater, reminders, Do Not Disturb, screening, or automatic prioritization.
  • You use Gmail, Microsoft 365, iCloud, Yahoo, Fastmail, IMAP, Exchange, or ActiveSync.

Choose an email-marketing platform when…

  • You need to collect subscribers and send campaigns.
  • You need landing pages, autoresponders, segmentation, or marketing automation.
  • Your goal is audience growth or sales rather than personal inbox triage.
