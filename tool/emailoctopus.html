Who EmailOctopus fits

  • You want a permanent free email-marketing plan rather than a short trial.
  • Your list is at or below 2,500 subscribers and 10,000 monthly sends are enough.
  • You need campaigns, basic automation, a form and a landing page without paying first.
  • You accept EmailOctopus branding while using the free tier.

Affiliate status

EmailOctopus publicly offers a free affiliate account with 30% revenue share for one year and a 31-day cookie.
