When Free is enough

  • You are testing Omnisend before committing money.
  • Your active sending audience can stay within 250 contacts.
  • 500 emails per month is enough for your current cadence.
  • You mainly need email, forms and automation rather than SMS.

When to consider paid

  • Your billable audience or monthly email volume exceeds the Free limits.
  • You need more predictable campaign capacity as the store grows.
  • You need Pro-only capabilities such as current SMS access for newer subscriptions.
  • The three-month starter offer materially lowers the cost of a real launch test.
Pricing watch-out

Your real bill depends on billable contacts

The $16 and $59 figures are starting prices, not a promise that every account will pay those amounts. Omnisend scales paid pricing with billable contacts, so check the live calculator before upgrading. List growth from forms, checkout and integrations can increase the billable audience over time.

SMS changed in 2026: for customers subscribing to a paid plan on or after May 4, 2026, SMS is available on Pro as an add-on rather than being included in the base subscription. Current US/Canada volume pricing starts at $0.007 per SMS according to Omnisend's help documentation.

Need stronger on-site pop-ups and capture?

Omnisend is the lower-risk start for a small list because it has a permanent Free plan. Privy is worth comparing when pop-ups, displays and on-site capture are the main job.
