What you are paying for

  • AI-assisted content generation and 40+ writing templates.
  • Keyword research and competitor SERP analysis.
  • Blog Wizard plus related, NLP and semantic keyword analysis.
  • Topical maps and SEO page/keyword tracking.
  • Newer answer-first tools for AI Overviews, FAQs and local-service content.

Check before upgrading

  • If you only need occasional AI drafting, the Free plan may be enough.
  • Higher plans are most useful when SEO analysis quotas, not just AI words, are the bottleneck.
  • Annual and promotional discounts move; verify the checkout total rather than relying on an old screenshot.
  • Run one real keyword-to-published-article workflow before buying a larger plan.

Lowest-risk buying path

Start free and run one representative article through keyword research, SERP analysis, drafting and optimization. If 2,500 words or the free analysis quotas are too restrictive, Starter is the lowest paid step. Writer is the more practical jump for regular publishing because it raises both word and SEO-analysis limits without moving straight to Pro pricing.
