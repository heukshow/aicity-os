FAQ

Is the free plan really free?

AiAssistWorks says Lite is free forever for light users, currently with 100 execution credits per month. You still supply your own AI-provider API key.

Does Plus include unlimited AI-model usage?

Plus removes AiAssistWorks execution-credit limits, but your AI provider and Google Workspace can still impose their own limits and charges.

What is the main tradeoff?

The software fee is low, but it is not an all-inclusive AI subscription. You must manage and pay for model usage separately through your API provider.

Start with the free plan, then upgrade only if the workflow earns its keep.
