Which VidIQ plan makes sense?

Start with Free

Best if you are validating VidIQ before paying. Current official documentation lists 150 AI credits per month, 3 keyword results per search, up to 3 tracked competitors and 3 personalized daily ideas.

Move to Boost when research limits slow you down

Boost raises the monthly AI allowance to 2,000 credits for one channel and unlocks unlimited keyword research, unlimited Outliers research, 50 personalized daily ideas, up to 20 tracked competitors, Best Time to Post and subscriber analysis.

Choose Max for heavier AI use

Max starts at 6,000 AI credits for one channel and raises competitor tracking to up to 50. It is the better fit when AI Coach, thumbnails, clipping, scripts and other credit-using tools become part of your regular workflow.
