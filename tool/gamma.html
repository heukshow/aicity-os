Gamma FAQ

Can I use Gamma without a credit card?

Yes. Gamma's current official pricing page states that no credit card is required and offers a Free plan.

Can Gamma export to PowerPoint?

Gamma lists PPTX among its export formats on the Free plan. It also lists PDF, PNG and Google Slides exports.

What is the practical difference between Plus and Pro?

Plus mainly expands generation and removes Gamma branding. Pro adds premium image models plus business-oriented controls such as custom branding/fonts, detailed analytics, advanced sharing, custom domains and API access.
