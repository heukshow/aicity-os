Free entry depends on where you use Adoric

Adoric's non-Shopify documentation currently lists a Free plan capped at 5,000 pageviews per month. The Shopify-specific popup product uses a separate 7-day trial and order-based paid model, so those offers should not be treated as the same plan.

Where Adoric fits

  • Popup, form and onsite lead-capture campaigns.
  • Behavioral targeting and personalization.
  • A/B testing and conversion experiments.
  • Gamified campaigns and product recommendations.
  • Shopify-focused upsell and ecommerce conversion workflows.

Adoric or Poptin?

Both support popup-driven onsite conversion and both provide a no-cost starting path for non-Shopify users, but their published limits use different units: Adoric documents pageviews while Poptin documents visitors. Compare workflow, features and the limit that matches your traffic model.
