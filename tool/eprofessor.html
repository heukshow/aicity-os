Payment setup matters if you plan to sell courses

eProfessor currently supports Stripe and Paddle for customer payments. Its documentation lists Stripe at 2.9% + 30¢ per successful charge and Paddle at 5% + 50¢, with Paddle acting as merchant of record for tax handling. These are processor terms described by eProfessor; verify current processor pricing before launch.

If you are only using eProfessor for internal training, this payment setup is not part of the core learner workflow.

Bottom line

eProfessor is most compelling when teaching and testing are the core job: employee training, certification, education, paid courses or digital learning products.
