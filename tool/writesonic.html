Writesonic FAQ

How much does Writesonic cost?

The current annual-billing view starts at $79/month for Starter, then $199/month for Basic and $399/month for Growth. Enterprise is custom. Official documentation lists month-to-month Basic at $249 and Growth at $499.

Is Writesonic still mainly an AI writing tool?

No. Content creation remains part of the product, but the current platform positions itself around AI search visibility, SEO and GEO as a unified marketing workflow.

Which AI platforms can Writesonic track?

The current self-serve pricing comparison lists ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. Enterprise expands coverage across all 10 platforms shown by Writesonic.

Is there a free trial?

Writesonic currently advertises a 7-day free trial and says no credit card is needed to start it.
