What you are actually paying for

AI app building: Taskade Genesis can create hosted apps, dashboards, websites, portals and internal tools from prompts while staying connected to workspace data.
AI agents: Pro supports unlimited agents that can use workspace knowledge, tools and integrations, including background execution.
Always-on automation: workflows can run on schedules even when you are offline and can connect agents, projects and external services.
Business controls: Business makes more sense when unlimited users, custom domains, analytics and white-labeling matter more than the lowest possible price.

Choose Taskade if...

  • You want apps, agents and automation in one workspace instead of separate subscriptions.
  • You want to test the product for free before committing to a paid plan.
  • Your small team can fit inside Pro's 10-user allowance.
  • You want AI agents and scheduled workflows that can keep working in the background.

Move to Business when...

  • You need more than 10 users or 50,000 monthly AI credits.
  • You need a custom domain or a more white-labeled client experience.
  • Advanced analytics and organization-level controls are operational requirements.
  • The extra capabilities solve a recurring business need, not just a one-time experiment.

Lowest-risk way to decide

Start on Free and build one representative Genesis app or agent workflow. Move to Pro only if unlimited apps and agents, scheduled automation, 50,000 monthly credits and the 10-user allowance solve a recurring need. Move to Business only when the larger credit pool, unlimited users, custom domains or white-label controls justify the higher cost.
