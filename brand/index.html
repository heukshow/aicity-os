Official brand identity

Better tools.
A brighter tomorrow.

COSHUMA is an evidence-first AI and SaaS discovery platform. We help people compare tools, understand trade-offs, verify pricing and trial information, and move from too many possibilities to a clearer next decision.

The idea behind the mark

Many possibilities, one clearer move

The larger blue form represents the wide field of software choices. The smaller light form represents a focused next move emerging from that field. Both point forward, expressing progress without using a literal arrow or letterform.

Designed around the product, not the spelling

The symbol is intentionally independent from the letters in COSHUMA. It is built around what the product does: reduce complexity, improve comparison, and help people choose with more confidence.

Clarity over hype

The mark uses a restrained system of deep navy, electric blue, cyan and white. It is designed to feel modern and optimistic without relying on generic AI imagery, metallic effects or decorative 3D styling.

Built to scale

The same symbol is designed to work in a browser tab, social profile, site header, document cover, business communication and large-format brand use.

Brand personality

Useful

COSHUMA should make software buying easier, not noisier.

Independent

Editorial comparison, pricing evidence and partner destinations are kept distinct.

Clear

Language should be direct, specific and easy to verify.

Forward-looking

The brand should feel current without chasing short-lived visual trends.

Usage rules

Do

Use the official SVG originals. Keep clear space around the mark. Use the light lockup on dark surfaces and the dark lockup on light surfaces. Preserve the original proportions.

Do not

Do not stretch, rotate, recolor with unrelated colors, add shadows, rebuild the symbol from letters, or place the logo on visually noisy backgrounds without adequate contrast.
