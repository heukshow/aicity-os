The real difference is workflow, not just price

AiAssistWorks

Best thought of as an AI layer over Google Workspace. The vendor says it supports 100+ AI models and can handle bulk spreadsheet tasks, document generation and slide generation. Because the API key is yours, you retain more direct control over model choice and model-billing cost.

Notion

Best thought of as the workspace itself. The current Business plan highlights Notion Agent, AI meeting notes, connected-app search, SAML SSO and granular database permissions alongside the underlying docs and database platform.

What should you test before paying?

Test AiAssistWorks with a real spreadsheet

Start on Lite. Run one repetitive task you already do manually, such as categorizing rows, rewriting product text, extracting fields or generating a document from structured data. Measure time saved and API spend before buying Plus.

Test Notion with a real team workflow

Rebuild one small workflow involving docs, a database and meeting follow-up. The $20/member/month Business price becomes easier to justify only when the workspace consolidation and AI features replace enough separate work.

Do not compare the subscription numbers alone. AiAssistWorks can have an additional model-API bill. Notion charges per member. The cheaper option depends on user count, AI volume and whether you are replacing other software.

Who should not buy either one?

If you only need occasional standalone AI chat, neither product may be the best first purchase. AiAssistWorks is valuable when Google Workspace automation is the job; Notion is valuable when the workspace itself is part of the problem. Start with a free tier or existing tools before adding another subscription.

Decision

For Google Sheets/Docs/Slides automation, AiAssistWorks is the more direct and lower-software-cost fit. For a company-wide workspace that combines knowledge, project data, meetings and AI agents, Notion is the broader product.
