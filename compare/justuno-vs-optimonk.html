Justuno vs OptiMonk

Both target onsite conversion, personalization and experimentation, but their no-cost evaluation models are materially different. Justuno gives a time-limited Lite trial; OptiMonk gives a permanent traffic-capped Free plan.

  • You can run a focused two-week evaluation.
  • Visitor identification and ecommerce recommendations are central to your workflow.
  • You are comfortable evaluating a paid entry tier after the trial.
