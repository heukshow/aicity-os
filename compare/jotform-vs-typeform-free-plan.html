Jotform vs Typeform free plan: which one gives you more room before paying?

The current free tiers are very different on raw response capacity. Jotform Starter includes 5 forms and 100 monthly submissions, while Typeform Free allows 10 responses per month. The right choice still depends on whether you care more about free-volume headroom, form style, logic, integrations, payment workflows or team needs.

Fast buyer decision

Choose Jotform first when…

You want substantially more free monthly submission capacity, up to five forms, and access to Jotform's standard form-building ecosystem before paying. Jotform says Starter includes standard features such as templates, payment integrations and widgets, with usage limits and Jotform branding.

Choose Typeform first when…

You want to test Typeform's form experience and its Free-plan features such as Logic, URL parameters, custom endings, follow-up messages, embedding and spreadsheet export, and 10 responses per month is enough for your initial validation.

What to test before upgrading

Build the same real workflow in both products: one lead form, one conditional path, one notification flow and one embedded version on your site. Compare completion rate, setup time, mobile behavior, integrations, export quality and how quickly you hit the free response limit.

Does Jotform's higher free response limit automatically make it better?

No. It gives you more free submission headroom, but form experience, design preferences, logic, integrations and downstream workflow matter. Typeform can still be the better fit for teams that prefer its interaction model and only need a small number of test responses.

Can Typeform Free users publish forms that use paid features?

Typeform's current help center says you can preview several paid features in a Free account, but a form using those paid features is closed to everyone except you until those features are removed or you upgrade.
