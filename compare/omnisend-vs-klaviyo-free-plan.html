Omnisend vs Klaviyo free plan: which ecommerce email platform is easier to test?

Both vendors currently let small ecommerce teams start at $0 with up to 250 contacts/profiles and 500 emails per month. The meaningful differences begin with how each product handles channels, support and the path from a free account to paid usage.

Fast buyer decision

Choose Omnisend first when…

You want a no-card free starting point and a clearly published paid ladder for ecommerce messaging. Omnisend's current documentation lists a Free plan at $0, Standard from $16/month and Pro from $59/month, with paid pricing scaling by billable contacts.

Choose Klaviyo first when…

You want to evaluate Klaviyo's ecommerce data, segmentation, automations and reporting ecosystem on a small list before deciding whether its profile-based paid pricing fits your store. Klaviyo's current Free plan supports up to 250 active profiles and 500 emails/month.

What to test before upgrading

Use the same small ecommerce list in your evaluation criteria: build a signup form, welcome automation, abandoned-cart flow and one promotional campaign. Compare setup time, segmentation, reporting, integration quality and how each platform counts contacts or active profiles before moving your full customer database.

Do equal free limits mean the products cost the same later?

No. The current free limits happen to be similar, but paid billing rules differ. Omnisend publishes Standard and Pro starting prices and adjusts tiers with billable contacts. Klaviyo's paid price depends on your active profile count and selected messaging products. Estimate the paid bill using each vendor's live calculator before choosing based on free limits alone.

Which platform will produce more revenue?

This page does not claim a revenue winner. Your conversion rate depends on audience quality, deliverability, offer, store integration, automation design and many other factors. Compare your own completed purchases, revenue per recipient, unsubscribe rate and time spent managing the platform.
