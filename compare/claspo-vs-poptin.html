Claspo vs Poptin

Both let you start with a permanent free tier. The more useful comparison is how their traffic limits are measured and whether the editor, targeting and widget workflow fits your site.

Decision pointClaspoPoptin
Free entry$0/month, no time limitFree Forever
Published free traffic limit3K pageviews/month1,000 visitors/month
Important measurement noteCounts pageviewsCounts visitors

Choose Claspo when…

  • The current 3K-pageview Free allowance better matches your test site.
  • You want unlimited widgets/team members on the published Free tier.
  • You are comfortable keeping the Powered by Claspo badge while testing.
Check Claspo pricing →

Choose Poptin when…

  • You prefer Poptin's popup/editor workflow.
  • A visitor-based free allowance is easier to map to your analytics.
  • You want its existing free-forever entry without a card.
