Landing-page platform comparison

Landingi vs Unbounce: choose by your campaign workflow

This is a direct comparison: both products are built around landing pages and conversion work. The useful distinction is how your team publishes, tests and scales campaigns rather than which product has the longer feature list.

Start with Landingi when

  • You want a dedicated visual landing-page builder with AI-assisted page generation.
  • Your plan decision depends on page and traffic limits, agency workflows or programmatic landing pages.
  • You want to test Landingi's EventTracker and server-side A/B testing on the plans that include them.
  • A 14-day trial is enough to validate the page-building workflow before committing.
Check Landingi trial & pricing →

Compare Unbounce when

  • Your buying process is centered on dedicated conversion optimization and landing-page experimentation.
  • Smart Traffic and Unbounce's CRO workflow are more important than Landingi's page-volume or agency model.
