Merlin wins if…

  • You want access to several major AI model families.
  • You summarize webpages, YouTube videos or documents frequently.
  • You want AI inside Chrome/browser workflows.
  • You are not committed to one workspace for all context.

Notion AI wins if…

  • Your company knowledge already lives in Notion.
  • You want AI meeting notes, research and database work in the same workspace.
  • You need AI that can search connected work information with workspace context.
  • Your team is already considering Business or Enterprise for collaboration features.

Best next step

If you are evaluating a standalone AI assistant, start with Merlin's free plan and test your actual browser/research tasks. If your AI value depends on team docs, projects and shared knowledge, inspect Notion AI inside the workspace where that context already exists.
