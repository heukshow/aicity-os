Privy vs Omnisend Pricing 2026: $30 vs Free/$16/$59

Both products cover ecommerce retention, but the lowest-risk starting point differs. Omnisend offers a permanent Free tier for small lists, while Privy starts with a 15-day no-card trial and puts pop-up capture, email and SMS in one paid stack.

Which should you shortlist?

Privy

Shortlist Privy when your immediate problem is converting store traffic with pop-ups and displays and you want email/SMS plus on-site capture under one vendor. Privy's Email plan currently starts at $30/month; Pop-ups & Displays starts at $24/month.

Omnisend

Shortlist Omnisend when you want to test ecommerce email and automation with no subscription cost first. Its Free plan supports up to 250 contacts and 500 emails/month; Standard starts at $16/month and Pro at $59/month.

What to test before paying

Compare the same contact count, monthly email volume, SMS destination countries and store traffic. Then test a signup form, welcome sequence and cart-recovery flow. Review mobile display behavior, suppression rules and how each platform counts billable contacts before importing your full list.

Do I need to replace my email platform to use Privy?

Privy lists a separate Pop-ups & Displays product that can sync collected contacts to Shopify, BigCommerce, Wix and selected third-party platforms. Confirm your existing platform is supported before selecting it.

Which will generate more sales?

This comparison does not establish a conversion or deliverability winner. Measure completed purchases, revenue per recipient and unsubscribes with your own audience; vendor features alone cannot predict the result.
