RGE Studio vs Stripo: which email-production workflow fits you?

RGE Studio (formerly Beefree) is a strong fit when visual email creation, reusable workspaces, approvals and broader team governance are the priority. Stripo is a strong fit when you want clearly defined export, testing and project allowances across a wide range of paid plans. Both provide a permanent free starting point, so test your real production workflow before upgrading.

The fastest way to choose

Choose RGE Studio first when…
  • You want a visual builder with a $0 Starter plan before paying.
  • Your workflow may grow into approvals, workspace permissions or larger-team governance.
  • You want to test Professional or Business without entering a credit card, subject to current trial eligibility.
Choose Stripo first when…
  • You want a free plan with explicit monthly export and email-storage limits.
  • You need predictable steps from Free to Basic, Medium or Pro as production volume rises.
  • You value a dedicated email-production tool with granular testing, export and project allowances.

RGE Studio: start free, then test premium collaboration

The official RGE Studio help center lists Starter at $0, Professional from $30/month and Business from $160/month. Its trial guidance says eligible Professional and Business trials do not require a credit card. If your team needs reusable email design plus workspace controls, measure whether those collaboration features remove enough production friction before upgrading.

Stripo: scale by exports, tests and projects

Stripo's current public pricing lists Free at $0, Basic at $20/month, Medium at $45/month and Pro at $125/month, with lower effective pricing when billed annually. The free tier currently includes two members, four exports per month and storage for ten emails. Use those limits to test your workflow before moving to a paid tier.
