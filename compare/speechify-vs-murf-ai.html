Speechify vs Murf AI

These products overlap on AI speech, but their strongest buyer use cases are different. Speechify is primarily useful when you want to listen to text and documents. Murf AI is a better fit when you need to produce generated voiceovers for content, training or marketing.

Choose Speechify when

You want text read aloud

Speechify's current consumer product focuses on reading text aloud, scanning and listening, voice typing, AI summaries and chat. Its Premium plan also advertises more than 1,000 natural voices and support for more than 60 languages.

Current public pricing
Free: $0 with basic read-aloud features and 10 robotic-style voices. Premium: $29/month on the monthly pricing view. Speechify also advertises a discounted annual option; verify the live checkout before purchase.
Check Speechify pricing →
Choose Murf AI when

You need generated voiceovers

Murf AI is positioned around AI voice generation for creators and businesses. Its Studio plans support voiceover production, commercial usage on paid tiers and a free entry point for testing the workflow.

Current public pricing
Free: $0 with no credit card required. Creator: $19/month when billed annually. Business: $66/month when billed annually. Murf's free workspace includes limited generation and does not include downloads or commercial rights.
