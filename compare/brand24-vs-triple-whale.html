Brand24 vs Triple Whale

These tools solve different growth problems. Brand24 is built to find and analyze online conversations about a brand, product, competitor, or keyword. Triple Whale is built to unify ecommerce performance data, attribution, and operating analytics.

Try Brand24 free for 14 days → Check Triple Whale pricing →

Brand24's official pricing page states that its 14-day trial does not require a credit card. Triple Whale has a free plan; paid pricing scales by business revenue tier.

Fast decision

Official vendor data checked Sep 4, 2026

Choose Brand24 if...

  • ✓ You need brand, product, or competitor mention monitoring.
  • ✓ Sentiment analysis and reputation signals matter.
  • ✓ PR, social, or marketing teams need alerts and coverage context.
  • ✓ You want to test the workflow before paying.

Choose Triple Whale if...

  • ✓ You operate an ecommerce business and need unified performance data.
  • ✓ Attribution, ROAS, first-party data, and post-purchase surveys matter.
  • ✓ Your team wants ecommerce dashboards plus an AI operator workflow.
  • ✓ You need a free analytics entry point before evaluating paid tiers.

Bottom line

If the question is “What are people saying about us, where, and with what sentiment?”, start with Brand24. If the question is “Which ecommerce channels and customer journeys are producing revenue?”, Triple Whale is the more direct fit. Some ecommerce teams can use both because they measure different parts of the growth loop.
