Plerdy vs Adoric

This is less about picking two identical popup tools and more about choosing your starting problem: diagnose behavior and UX with Plerdy, or run targeted onsite conversion campaigns with Adoric.

Decision pointPlerdyAdoric
Core strengthHeatmaps, recordings, SEO and CRO analyticsPopups, targeting, personalization, gamification
Free entry$0 / Forever planNon-Shopify Free plan; Shopify path has 7-day trial
Best first questionWhy are visitors struggling or dropping?What onsite message or offer should each visitor see?

Choose Plerdy when…

  • You need heatmaps and session replay before changing the site.
  • You want SEO auditing and conversion analysis in one stack.
  • You prefer a permanent free starting tier.

Choose Adoric when…

  • Gamification or ecommerce recommendations matter.
  • You are evaluating its Shopify-specific conversion workflow.
