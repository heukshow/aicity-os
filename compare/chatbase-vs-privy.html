Chatbase vs Privy

These tools solve different jobs. Chatbase builds AI agents for customer support and customer-facing conversations. Privy focuses on ecommerce email, SMS, pop-ups and conversion campaigns.

Choose Chatbase when…

Support questions are the bottleneck

  • ✓ You want an AI agent trained on business knowledge.
  • ✓ You need customer conversations across chat and other support channels.
  • ✓ You want to automate repetitive support before adding more human handling.
Current entry point: Free at $0/month with 50 message credits/month. Hobby is $40/month and currently offers a 7-day trial.

Choose Privy when…

Store conversion and retention are the bottleneck

  • ✓ You need ecommerce email and SMS campaigns.
  • ✓ You want pop-ups, targeting and segmentation.
  • ✓ You care more about capturing and converting shoppers than answering broad support questions.
Current entry point: Email starts at $30/month; Pop-ups & Displays starts at $24/month. Privy currently advertises a 15-day free trial with no credit card required.

Best low-risk test

If support automation is the problem, start with Chatbase's free tier and test it on a narrow set of real questions before paying. If ecommerce campaigns are the problem, use Privy's 15-day trial and measure captured leads, email engagement and attributable sales. Do not buy both just because the feature lists overlap.
