AWeber vs Moosend

AWeber

Clear tiered email-marketing plans

AWeber currently lists Lite starting at $15/month or $150/year and Plus starting at $30/month or $240/year, with pricing increasing by subscriber tier. Its pricing page advertises a 14-day free trial.

  • ✓ Lite: focused core email tools with quantity limits
  • ✓ Plus: unlimited lists, landing pages and automations
  • ✓ Detailed published subscriber/send tiers
  • ✓ Strong fit when you want predictable feature tiers

Moosend

Longer no-card trial + flexible billing

Moosend currently offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card required. Its live pricing scales by active contacts across Pro, Moosend+ and Enterprise; Moosend's own current guidance describes paid plans as starting at $9/month.

  • ✓ 30-day no-card trial
  • ✓ Unlimited campaigns/sends shown for paid plan comparison
  • ✓ 15% biannual and 20% annual savings currently advertised
  • ✓ Email credits available for occasional senders

Which should you pick?

Choose AWeber if you prefer its published feature tiers and want a mature email-marketing platform with clear Lite/Plus limits. Choose Moosend if the longer no-card trial, flexible billing cadence or occasional-send credits reduce your initial commitment. For either one, price your real subscriber count before paying.
