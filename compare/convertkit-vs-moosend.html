Kit (formerly ConvertKit) vs Moosend

The key difference is not just price: Kit is built around creator audience growth and monetization, while Moosend is positioned as an email-marketing and automation stack for broader business workflows.

KIT

Best when a permanent free creator plan matters

Kit currently offers Free at $0 for up to 10,000 subscribers. For 1,000 subscribers, its official pricing page currently shows Creator at $33/month on annual billing and Pro at $66/month on annual billing.

  • ✓ Unlimited forms, landing pages and broadcasts on Free
  • ✓ Digital products and paid subscriptions
  • ✓ Creator Network and audience-growth features
  • ✓ Paid tiers unlock visual automations and sequences

MOOSEND

Best when you can use 30 days to test a business workflow

Moosend currently offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card required. Its paid pricing scales by active contacts across Pro, Moosend+ and Enterprise, while current Moosend guidance describes paid plans as starting at $9/month.

  • ✓ Campaigns and marketing automation
  • ✓ Landing pages and subscription forms
  • ✓ 15% biannual / 20% annual savings currently advertised
  • ✓ Email credits available for occasional campaigns
