Popup & onsite conversion comparison

Adoric vs Poptin

Both tools target onsite conversion, but their free-entry models and product emphasis differ. The biggest trap is comparing their headline limits directly: Adoric publishes pageviews for its non-Shopify Free plan, while Poptin publishes visitors.

Decision pointAdoricPoptin
Free entryNon-Shopify Free planFree Forever plan
Published free limitUp to 5,000 pageviews/monthUp to 1,000 visitors/month
Shopify pathSeparate 7-day popup trial, then order-based pricingGeneral popup platform; check current Shopify/integration support
Core conversion workflowPopups, targeting, personalization, gamification, recommendationsPopups, forms, coupons, targeting, A/B testing, email automation

Choose Adoric when…

  • You want Adoric's personalization, gamification or product-recommendation stack.
  • You are evaluating its Shopify-specific ecommerce workflow.
  • Your non-Shopify traffic fits a pageview-based free limit.
Visit Adoric →

Choose Poptin when…

  • You want a Free Forever entry tier measured by visitors.
  • Email campaigns and automation inside the same popup platform matter.
  • You want a broad popup/form workflow before moving to paid traffic limits.
Visit Poptin →

Important: pageviews ≠ visitors

A single visitor can generate several pageviews, so 5,000 pageviews cannot be treated as five times 1,000 visitors. Use your own analytics to estimate which free limit is actually more generous for your site.
