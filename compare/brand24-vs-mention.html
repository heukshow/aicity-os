Brand24 vs Mention: which social-listening route fits your team?

Brand24 and Mention both cover social and web monitoring, but they are no longer equivalent buying options for a new customer. Brand24 is sold as a standalone product with published plans and a 14-day no-card trial. Mention’s help center states that, as of July 1, 2026, Mention is no longer sold as a standalone product; existing customers can keep their plans, while new social-listening evaluations are directed toward Agorapulse Listening.

Brand24

For teams that want a standalone, self-serve option

Brand24 currently offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. Its Individual plan is listed at $249/month on monthly billing or $199/month when billed annually.

Brand24 is the more relevant route when…

  • You are shopping for a new standalone social-listening subscription today.
  • You want published plan pricing and a defined 14-day no-card trial before paying.
  • You want to test keyword monitoring, alerts, sentiment and competitor tracking without first moving into a sales-led evaluation.

Mention is still relevant when…

  • You are an existing Mention customer deciding whether to keep your current plan.
  • You need to understand Mention’s documented monitoring, Boolean search, reporting and collaboration capabilities before evaluating the Agorapulse Listening path.
  • You are comparing migration or successor options rather than shopping for a new standalone Mention subscription.

Lowest-risk way to decide

If you already have access to Mention, use the same monitoring brief in both products: one brand name, one competitor and one important category keyword. Compare relevant-result quality, alert usefulness, analysis depth and reporting. If you are a new buyer, first confirm which products are actually available for a new subscription before comparing trial experience.
