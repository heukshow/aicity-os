Shopify vs Reply.io

These products support different revenue motions. Shopify is for selling products through an ecommerce storefront and checkout. Reply.io is for prospecting and outbound B2B sales outreach across email and other channels. Pick the revenue engine you need before comparing price.

ECOMMERCE REVENUE

Choose Shopify when customers should buy through a store

  • ✓ Storefront, products, checkout, payments and inventory in one hosted platform.
  • ✓ Suitable for direct-to-consumer, retail, social and marketplace commerce.
  • ✓ Built around orders and checkout conversion rather than outbound prospecting sequences.
  • ✓ Best first choice when you need a real ecommerce transaction layer.
Current US entry offer
3 days free, then $1/month for 3 months. Yearly Basic pricing currently starts at $29/month.

B2B OUTBOUND REVENUE

Choose Reply.io when your team needs to reach prospects

  • ✓ Email outreach automation with warm-up and deliverability tools.
  • ✓ Multichannel sequences can add LinkedIn, calls, SMS and other steps.
  • ✓ Built-in live B2B data and AI-assisted outreach features.
  • ✓ Better fit for lead generation and sales meetings than ecommerce checkout.

Fast decision: which revenue model are you building?

Visitors should place orders

Choose Shopify when the core action is adding products to cart and completing checkout.

Sales reps should book meetings

Choose Reply.io when the core action is contacting prospects and moving them into a B2B sales conversation.

Hybrid business

A company can use both, but only if it runs both ecommerce and outbound sales motions. Do not buy both simply because both are labeled sales tools.

Need an online store? Test checkout before adding more software

A working product page and checkout are the first proof points for ecommerce. Start with the current Shopify trial path and validate that flow before expanding the stack.
