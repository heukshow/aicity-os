The faster decision rule

If your main pain is operational—documents sitting unsigned, repeated reminders, printing and scanning—test the signing workflow that removes those steps fastest. If the issue is enterprise standardization or existing integrations, the familiar platform may still be the lower-risk choice even at a higher headline cost.
